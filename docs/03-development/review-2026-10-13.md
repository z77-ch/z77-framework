## 2. Medium

- **Collective account 1100 ≠ open items** in the dev DB by 108.10 — seven manual entries (Nr. 4–12, 2026-09-23) post straight to 1100; nothing prevents or reports it, so the P4 exit criterion is not enforced. Fix: an account flag «nur durch Modul bebuchbar» refused by the manual forms, plus a reconciliation check under `period-close`. **Owner decision.**
- **PDF of journal / account statement hydrates everything** (`PDF_ALL_ROWS`, `ReportPdf.php:163`, `counterAccounts()` with an `IN` of all ids) — fails at P5b volumes. Fix: page-wise reading with `clear()` or a SQL stream.
- **`StatementComparison` sorts by account number** (`:59`), not by the chart tree; `unplaced` accounts are no longer shown though financial.md says so. Fix: keep the tree order; show `unplaced`.
- **Dunning has no minimum interval between levels and no backdating limit** (run 4 dated 23.09. created 13.10., run 5 at level 2 two minutes later). **Owner decision**: next level not before N days after the last notice; no notice date before the last run.
- **«Debitor» switch off deletes the profile with one click** (session CSRF, no confirmation) — against fetch.md («toggles are non-destructive»). Fix: confirmation dialog with entity token.
- **Role link «Debitor bearbeiten …» replaces the open contact dialog** — unsaved contact fields are lost. Fix: open as a window.
- **Windows ignore HTTP status / JSON** (`core.js:1010-1037`): an expired session or 403/500 lands as text in the window. Fix: route `!ok` / JSON through `handleEnvelope`.
- **Region load race** (`core.js:786-809`) and **region swap not wired** (`:796`). Fix: sequence number / AbortController; `wire()` after the swap.
- **Debug error handler renders HTML into fetch answers** (`Functions.php:181`) — the real cause of the PNG upload error; any future notice breaks JSON in debug mode. Fix: log (or put into the envelope) in fetch mode.
- **Inline handlers** (`StatsController/listAction.tpl.php:50`, `_report.tpl.php:264`) — Rule 7 / CSP. Fix: delegated triggers.
- **A draft with a deactivated payment target can be saved but never booked**: `reinvoice` keeps the inactive target (`InvoicingService.php:693`, harness P3C17), the booking print refuses it (`:732`). Fix: make both sides equal (drop the re-issue exception).
- **ADR-040 decision 3** still says «re-issued under the same number». Fix: addendum (drafts have no number since 2026-10-10).

## 7. Blockers for P5 part 2 / year-end (input for the P5 plan)

1. `Period` cannot reach `vat-settled`; close/reopen overwrite the single `state` — the settled periods are lost. Needs a `VatReturn` entity (or a separate column).
2. Tax lines into settled periods are still refused (`PostingRules.php:90,111`, `ManualEntryService`) — against the owner decision of 2026-09-30.
3. `Mandator::ACCOUNT_KEYS` lacks the VAT settlement, VAT rounding, retained earnings and annual result accounts.
4. Lock order: `close()` holds the years exclusively, `post()` takes the range first — an opening entry inside `close()` would invert it. Year-end as its own unit of work before the close.
5. Year-end entries must precede the close; nothing checks year N+1 exists or that its opening is current after late postings (`amend()` with `opening:{code}` could carry it).
6. `EntryKind` cannot tell closing entries apart (class 9 would zero the income statement). Decide: result only via the opening entry (2970/2979) or a filter by kind/source.
7. Class 9 has no postable accounts and existing installations have no path to add them (`adoptKmuChart()` refuses a non-empty chart).
8. Comparison columns stay misleading until the opening entry exists; `type_locked` (FIN-TYPE-001) is still open.
