## mental model

The bank account service (ADR-060) keeps the bank's account statement in z77; built today (B1) it is an INFORMATION SOURCE: when was an invoice paid, and how many CHF did the bank debit for it. Decided 2026-11-17 (ADR-061), stages C1–C5 built the same day: BANK-DRIVEN BOOKKEEPING — on a switched-on account, from its date «Abgleich ab», every movement is «geschäftlich», «privat» or «zu prüfen», and the ledger account's change must equal the business movements (stages C1–C5, `## pending`). A camt.053 file becomes a `BankStatement` with its `BankMovement`s and goes unaltered into an archive; a movement never posts BY ITSELF, and its state is derived, never stored on it (stage C2). What is stored for a bank account is ONE SEAMLESS CHAIN — a file that does not OVERLAP the last stored day, or whose overlap does not agree with what is stored, is refused. The service lives in module-financial (`Z77\Module\Financial\Bank\`) and names no consumer. Stage B1 (2026-11-16, reworked the same day after the owner narrowed the purpose) built the master data «Bankkonten», the import and the archive as services with their harness; the three screens followed on 2026-11-17 after the owner approved the slim boards.

- **«Bankkonten»** (`BankAccount`, table `bank_account`, `BankAccountService`): `code` (unique, immutable — what a payment method names), `label`, `iban` (normalised, unique, immutable once a statement was imported), `currency` (the base currency; a new account gets it), `ledger_account` (a postable, active account), `active`, and ONE switch, OFF by default: `statement_import` («Kontoauszug einlesen»). Never deleted. It is the ONE place that ties an IBAN to a ledger account.
- **The parser is shared** (`Z77\Shared\Payment\Camt\CamtParser`, owner decision 12): pure data — `CamtMessage` (kind, message id) → `CamtReport` (`Stmt` / `Ntfctn` / `Rpt`: id, sequence numbers, period, IBAN, currency, `CamtBalance`s signed) → `CamtEntry` (`Ntry`) → `CamtTransaction` (`TxDtls`: references, amount, instructed amount with `CcyXchg`, `CamtParty` debtor / ultimate debtor / creditor / ultimate creditor with account IBAN, structured reference, message). Hardened XML (`LIBXML_NONET`, no entity loading). Debtor's `Payments\CamtReader` (camt.054) and financial's `Bank\Camt053Reader` are views of it; `CamtArchive` reads the XML files of a bank's ZIP with fixed limits (debtor's `BankImportArchive` delegates to it).
- **`Camt053Reader::read($xml)`** → one `StatementData` per `Stmt`, one `MovementData` per BOOKED entry (`Sts` = `BOOK`; others are counted in `skipped`). The ENTRY is the movement — it moves the balance. With exactly one transaction its counterparty (creditor of a debit, debtor of a credit, the ultimate party when it carries a name), the account IBAN, the QRR / SCOR reference and the instructed foreign amount with the bank's rate fill the movement; a collective entry keeps the entry's amount and text and counts its transactions (`tx_count`). The text is the remittance message plus `AddtlNtryInf` — on a card purchase the only place that names the merchant. Refused: not XML, not a camt.053, no `OPBD` / `CLBD`, an entry without a booking date or in another currency, and a statement that is not consistent in itself (opening + booked entries ≠ closing).
- **`BankStatementService::import($bytes, $fileName)`** → one `BankImportResult` per statement; all statements of a file in ONE unit of work, or none. Refusals with a German sentence (`BankRefusedException`, stable reason codes): the same file (SHA-256), the same message for the account (`MsgId` + `Stmt/Id`), an IBAN that is an INACTIVE bank account, «Kontoauszug einlesen» off, another currency than the base currency, and the three chain refusals below. A file of which NO statement belongs to a bank account of the installation is `not-bank-account` (the IBANs in `BankRefusedException::$subjects`) — the upload SKIPS it (point «Accounts that are no bank account» below). Lock order: the bank account rows (by id) first, then the reads, then the writes.
- **The seamless chain** (ADR-060 decision 18). The first statement of an account sets the start. From then on the account is «eingelesen bis D · Saldo C»: D = `to_date` of the NEWEST statement (the date of `FrToDt/ToDtTm`), C = its closing balance (`BankStatementService::coveredTo()`).
  - **A file must OVERLAP the last stored day (owner 2026-11-18, amending decision 18):** a statement starting after D → `no-overlap`, refused, both dates named and D as the day to download from («bitte einen Auszug ab dem D laden — die Überlappung wird erkannt»). That holds for a start on D + 1 too: D may have been exported during the day, and two bookings made on D afterwards that net to zero would pass every balance proof unseen. Ruled out with the owner: a weekend / holiday rule (an assumption — time zones, holidays, banks booking on Saturday), equal balances alone (+x / −x in the hole), and `ElctrncSeqNb` (BANK-SEQ-001).
  - **The one exception, inside a file:** a statement FOLLOWING another statement of the same bank account in the SAME file joins it when the earlier one reached D (`join(…, $reachedInFile)`) — a bank that sends one statement per booking day never overlaps itself, and inside one file the order is the bank's own. The balance proof below applies to every statement all the same.
  - The file's entries are split by the bank's entry reference: known (the account holds them) and new. The join is proven by the balance: C + new must equal the file's closing balance — the file being consistent in itself, that is «the file's balance at the covered end equals C». No match, or a file ending before D that brings anything new → `chain-mismatch`, refused, the three sums named.
  - A new movement dated before the account's start → `before-start`, refused.
  - New = nothing and the file ends on or before D → nothing is stored (no statement row, no archived file); the result says «Nichts Neues».
  - A file with no movement that ends after D and whose balance is C is stored: it moves the covered end.
- **Why the cut is by reference, not by date** (the rule for intraday `ToDtTm`): an export made during day D ends its period on D; the bank may book further entries on D afterwards. They carry new references, the next file brings them as new, and the balance proves the join. A cut «booking date > D» would lose them or refuse every later file.
- **Dedup key**: `bank_movement.dedup_key` is unique per bank account — the bank's `Ntry/AcctSvcrRef`, else `fp:` + a fingerprint (booking date, value date, side, amount, bank transaction code, text, rank among identical entries of the statement; `BankMovement::isFingerprinted()`). A known movement is never touched by a later file.
- **Balance per movement**: `bank_movement.balance` is the account's booked balance after the movement — C plus the new movements in file order; the chain being seamless, it is exact (`tests/financial-bank.php` I20 checks the running sum over all rows).
- **Accounts that are no bank account (owner 2026-11-18):** a bank's ZIP carries a camt.053 for EVERY account of the contract. The upload skips a file of such accounts and names them in ONE neutral line at the end of its answer — «4 Konten übersprungen (nicht als Bankkonto angelegt): CH.. …, …» — not silently (a forgotten business account must be noticed), not as an error (status `ok` when nothing else was refused). A file that MIXES known and unknown accounts, an inactive bank account and «Kontoauszug einlesen» off stay refusals with their sentence.
- **The answer of an upload** is `BankImportResult::message()`: «45 neue Bewegungen, Saldo stimmt (eingelesen bis 31.03.2026, Saldo …).» or «Nichts Neues — das Bankkonto … ist bereits eingelesen bis …».
- **The archive** (`BankStatementArchive`): `data/archive/financial/bank/<YYYY>/<YYYYMMDD>-<bank account code>-<sha256, 16 hex>.xml`, year and date of the (last) closing date in the file. It writes and reads through the shared `Z77\Shared\Files\WriteOnceStore` (kernel shared; switched 2026-11-17, behaviour unchanged): written inside the unit of work under a temporary name, read back and verified, then moved; an existing path with other bytes is refused; `bytes()` verifies the SHA-256 against the statement row (its caller: the download `statement` of the movement window). No delete path except `discardWritten()` after a rollback — and after a rollback `BankStatementService` discards only when NO committed statement row names a file of the same bytes: two imports of one file archive under the same path, the one that lost must not delete the winner's record (BANK-ARCHIVE-001). Not the DMS. Only a file that stored at least one statement is archived.
- **Built today** (B1, C1, C2, C3, C4): a movement can be linked (C1, next point), has a derived state and can be posted by an explicit act (C2, below); the switch + date, the anchor, the check and the close blocks are stage C4 (the points «Bank-driven = switch + date» … «The close» below).
- **Bank-driven = switch + date (C4, built 2026-11-17 — ADR-061 decisions 1–3, no modes)** — `BankAccount::$reconcileFrom` («Abgleich ab», `bank_account.reconcile_from`, nullable; `Version20261117200000`). `isBankDriven()` = «Kontoauszug einlesen» on AND the date set; `drives($day)` = bank-driven and `$day` ≥ the date; the SQL twin is `BankMovementRepository::DRIVEN`. Everything else is INFORMATION ONLY: a movement before the date, every movement of an account without the date, and every movement of an account switched off. Information only means: NO state (`STATE` answers `STATE_NONE` = ''; `stateCounts()`, `openPatternQuestions()`, `countPrivateBy()` leave it out), nothing can be decided (`BankMovementStates` and `BankPostingService::post()` refuse with `not-driven` and a German sentence; `takeBack()` stays possible — a correction), not in the check, no close block. A link CAN still be set on it (C1's `freeMovements()` knows only the switch — creditor's «Zahlungen suchen …» keeps working as an information source). Nothing is stored on a movement, so setting the date, moving it, or switching off and on changes what is shown at once and loses nothing (links, postings, marks, patterns, the anchor stay). The date is typed in the bank account window (`type="date"`; `setReconcileFrom()` reads `Y-m-d` or `d.m.Y`, the validator names anything else on the field); a migration never sets it — every existing account stays what it was.
- **The anchor (C4 — decisions 22, 24)** — `BankAnchor` (table `bank_anchor`, one row per bank account and day): the bank's balance, the ledger account's balance and their DIFFERENCE at the OPENING of a day, confirmed by the office. `Bank\BankReconciliation::anchor($account, $day)` computes «Bank X · Fibu Y · Differenz Z»: the bank's balance = the opening balance of the FIRST statement + every movement booked before the day (null with a sentence when the chain does not reach the day); the ledger balance = the lines of the ledger account in the fiscal year of the day dated before it, plus the year opening wherever it is dated. States: `open` (nothing confirmed — the day is «Abgleich ab»), `confirmed`, `computed` (a later day — the first day of the fiscal year the window shows — whose real difference equals the last confirmed one + the PRIVATE net since: nothing to do), `mismatch` (it does not — confirm again), `unread`. `confirm($account, $asOf, $difference)` stores it under the bank account's row lock and refuses when the figures moved since the office saw them, or when there is nothing to confirm; a day confirmed before whose figures changed is confirmed anew (its row is replaced). Works for a mixed account (difference ≠ 0 — the private history was never booked) and a purely business one (0.00) alike. The year-end posting «Privat / Bank» is NOT built (open with the fiduciary).
- **The check (C4 — decisions 23, 25)** — `BankReconciliation::check($account, $from, $to)` → `Bank\BankCheck` (computed, never stored; null when the account is not bank-driven or the range lies before «Abgleich ab»; a range across the date starts at it). It compares CHANGES: `ledger` = Σ (debit − credit) of the entries on the bank's ledger account dated in the range, the YEAR OPENING left out (an entry of origin `opening` or with a line on the mandator's opening contra account — `JournalLineRepository::changesOnAccount()`); `business` = Σ of the signed amounts of the «geschäftlich» movements booked in the range; `difference()` = ledger − business. It names: `entries` / `entryCount` — «Buchung ohne Bewegung», the entries whose record is linked to no movement of this account booked IN the range (an entry whose movement lies in another period carries «Bewegung am …» as its note; an entry and its reversal, both in the range and both without a movement, are no finding); `unbooked` — business movements of the range without an entry in it; `review` — movements «zu prüfen»; `isReadFrom()` / `isReadTo()` — the statement covers the range. `passes()` = `problems()` is empty; `problems()` are the German sentences the close yields.
- **Which entry belongs to which movement (C4 — Rule 9)** — financial knows a link only by its opaque owner key and an entry only by its opaque origin. Its OWN direct postings it joins itself (origin `bank`, idempotency key `bank-posting:<id>` → owner `posting:<id>`). Every other module answers through the registry `Bank\BankEntryOwners` (config key `bankEntryOwners`, a list of `BankEntryOwnerInterface` classes — the `openWorkChecks` pattern): `ownerKeys($entries)` → entry id → owner key. Creditor: `Services\CreditorBankEntryOwner` (origin `creditor` / invoice id → `creditor:{id}`, no read). Debtor: `Services\DebtorBankEntryOwner` (origin `payment` / payment id → the camt.054 entry `debtor:entry:{AcctSvcrRef}` of its bank transaction, else the day group). Receipt: `Services\ReceiptBankEntryOwner` (origin `receipt` / receipt id → `receipt:{id}`, no read). A module that declares nothing has its postings on a bank's ledger account reported as «Buchung ohne Bewegung» — honest, never silent.
- **The close (C4 — decisions 25, 26)** — `Close\BankReconciliationCheck` in `financialConfig → openWorkChecks` under `period-close` (the year close; a month close once one exists) and `vat-return`. For every ACTIVE bank-driven account whose driven time reaches into the range, BLOCKING: the statement not read from «Abgleich ab» / to the end of the range; movements «zu prüfen»; the check does not agree (difference, postings without a movement, business movements without a posting — one sentence); and once: the TRANSFER clearing account not at zero at the end of the range (`BankReconciliation::transferBalance()`). WARNINGS: at the VAT return «n Buchungen mit Vorsteuer ohne Beleg · CHF x Vorsteuer» (`taxWithoutVoucher()` — direct postings with a tax code and no archived voucher, not taken back, the tax = Σ `tax_amount` of the entry); at the year close an anchor that is not confirmed or no longer agrees. An account that is switched off or has no date says nothing.
- **«Privat» and the transfer account are the MANDATOR's (C4, decided 2026-11-17)** — `mandator.account_private` / `account_bank_transfer` (keys `private`, `bank-transfer`; Stammdaten › Firma › Mandant, Reiter «Konten»; `Version20261117190000` of module-mandator), read by the ONE reader `LedgerService::bankPostingAccount()`. They stood in `financialConfig → bankPosting` for stage C2 (never deployed) and moved because a module config override REPLACES the whole config (BOOT-CONFIG-001) — a project could not record «my transfer account is 1095» without copying every key —, and because every other account of the company already lives on the mandator record (Rule 2; the VAT accounts since E2, the year-opening accounts since 2026-10-22). NO start value and NO column default: the shipped chart holds neither account, and an installation's chart may use 1090 for something else — empty = «Privat» / «Übertrag eigenes Konto» are refused with a sentence naming the mandator, and the transfer check is skipped. `bankPosting` keeps only `voucherFromGross`.
- **The window «Abgleich …» (C4)** — the toolbar tool of «Bankkonto» for a bank-driven account (`reconcile?account=<code>&year=<code>`, `reconcile.tpl.php`); while the anchor is `open`, the list «Bewegungen» says so in ONE line above its rows («Per … : Bank … · Fibu … · Differenz …» with the BUTTON «Bestätigen …» into the window — a button, like «Entscheiden …» of an open pattern question: as a link at the end of the sentence it was not seen as clickable, owner 2026-11-18 — decision 22's «at the first import the bank account shows»). The window: the anchor line with its state and — only while there is something to confirm — «Differenz bestätigen» in the action row; one row per period of the fiscal year (Bank geschäftlich · Fibu · Differenz · Zu prüfen · Ohne Bewegung · Stand); the postings without a movement of the year as links into the journal (`detail?ref=`); eingelesen bis, the private sum, business movements without a posting, the transfer account's balance, «Vorsteuer ohne Beleg». Labels and figures only; `reconcile.help` explains.
- **The link (C1, built 2026-11-17)** — `BankLink` (table `bank_link`, `Version20261117100000`): `movement_id`, the owner as a KEY (`owner_type` + `owner_key` `creditor:17` + `owner_label` «Rechnung 4711 · Muster AG» — financial never resolves it, Rule 9), an `amount`, a `basis` (`reference` = identity, set without asking · `proposal` = a unique likeness the office confirmed · `manual` = picked among candidates), who / when. The rule of C1 — ONE link per movement over its FULL amount, one movement per owner — is two unique indexes plus `BankLinkService::link()`; n:1 / 1:n later drop an index, the table stays. **`BankLinkService`** is the port of the owning modules: `freeMovements()` / `freeMovement()` (FREE = unlinked, no reversal, an ACTIVE bank account with «Kontoauszug einlesen» on; by direction, references, booked amount, instructed amount + currency, booking-date window), `freeReferences()` and `firstFreeBooking()` (one read each, for a job and a bounded scan), `link()` — ONLY inside the owner's unit of work: the movement row is locked (`BankLinkRepository::lockMovement()`), what is linked is read after the lock, refusals `link-gone` / `link-taken` / `link-owner` / `link-amount` as `BankRefusedException` with a German sentence —, `release()` (in the owner's unit of work, with its own correction — ADR-061 decision 10), `relabel()`, `ofOwner()` / `ofOwners()` / `linkedOwners()` / `ofMovements()`. A link never posts and changes nothing on the movement. WHICH movement pays a record is the owner's judgement: creditor's `CreditorBankMatchService` (creditor.md «Bank-driven «Bezahlt am»») is the first owner; debtor (C3b, built 2026-11-17 — `Services\BankEntryLinkService`, owner `debtor:entry:{Ntry/AcctSvcrRef}` through `freeMovementsByEntryRefs()`, the bank's ENTRY reference, never `NtryRef`) is the second; receipt (C5, built 2026-11-17 — `Services\ReceiptBankMatchService`, owner `receipt:{id}`, label «Beleg <id>»; never automatic, a slip carries no reference — [`receipt.md`](receipt.md)) is the third.
- **The list «Bewegungen» since C1**: the column «Zuordnung» shows the link's `owner_label` (escaped; empty = nothing yet), the toolbar (hc2, `toolbar.tpl.php`) the views. The list sets and releases no link of ANOTHER module — that is the owner's screen (creditor: «Zahlungen suchen …», «Zuordnung lösen»). C1's view «Ohne Zuordnung» (`?link=free`) is gone: stage C2 replaced it by the states (next points; `?state=`, `BankMovementRepository::search()` / `countSearch()` with `$state`).
- **The state of a movement (C2, built 2026-11-17; under the switch + date since C4)** — «geschäftlich» / «privat» / «zu prüfen», DERIVED in one SQL expression (`BankMovementRepository::STATE`, read through `statesOf()`, `stateCounts()`, the `$state` filter of `search()`), never stored: (0) not bank-driven on its day → NONE (information only); (1) a LINK → business (a document beats a pattern, decision 7); (2) else the office's mark by hand (`BankMovementMark`, table `bank_movement_mark`, at most one per movement: `private` · `review` = held on «zu prüfen» although a private pattern takes it · `exception` = decision 8 answered «Nein», says nothing without a link); (3) else a PRIVATE pattern that takes it → private; (4) else «zu prüfen». Nothing is private by silence.
- **Learned patterns (C2)** — `BankPattern` (table `bank_pattern`), ONE per bank account, direction and `match_key`. The key is `BankPattern::keyOf()` of the movement's own data — the counterparty's IBAN, else the merchant after «Bezugsort:» (up to the comma), else the counterparty's name, else the text without its numbers; lower-cased, whitespace collapsed, '' when nothing is left — and is kept on the movement as `bank_movement.pattern_key` (derived data with an index, filled by the entity's constructor and, for older rows, by the migration's `postUp()` with a frozen copy of the rule; NOT a state). `kind`: `private` (every movement it takes is private, the coming ones too) · `mixed` («gemischt»: its movements are always «zu prüfen») · `business` (it only carries the proposal). Optional NARROWING, set when the pattern is confirmed: `amount_min` / `amount_max` (the confirmed amount ± 10 %) and `day_of_month` (± 3 days, across the month end) — the monthly pension, not every credit of the sender; `BankPattern::takes()` and the SQL fragment `TAKES` are twins. The PROPOSAL (`contra_account`, `tax_code`): what «Geschäftlich buchen …» last used for the key.
- **A module can CLAIM a movement (owner 2026-11-18)** — `Bank\BankMovementClaims` (config key `bankMovementClaims`, a list of `BankMovementClaimInterface` classes — the `bankAccountHints` pattern, Rule 9): `claimOn($movement)` answers a German sentence when the movement is that module's to link. `markPrivate()` refuses such a movement (`claimed`) — as a pattern AND by hand —, `markPrivateMany()` counts it apart with the sentence. Debtor declares `Services\CollectiveCreditBankClaim`: a credit the bank codes `PMNT/RCDT/VCOM` on a bank account whose IBAN an active payment target carries (plain or QR-IBAN) — the COLLECTIVE CREDIT of QR payments. Why: it has no counterparty, so its pattern key is its text without numbers («sammelgutschrift qrr v.»); ONE «Privat» made every collective credit private, the coming ones too, and a camt.054 nobody read would have vanished as private (found live 2026-11-18).
- **What the office decides (`Bank\BankMovementStates`, every write under the movement's row lock)**: `markPrivate()` — «Privat» confirms the PATTERN (a new one, or one that so far only carried a proposal; with `$nearAmount` / `$nearDay` narrowed); with `$onlyThis`, without a key, on a «gemischt» pattern or on a private pattern narrowed elsewhere it marks this ONE movement by hand. `markPrivateMany()` for ticked rows. `setReview()` — one movement back to «zu prüfen» (held by a mark where a private pattern takes it). `liftPattern()` — the private pattern taken back (removed, or reduced to its proposal). A linked movement is refused (`state-business`). DECISION 8: a movement that is LINKED although a private pattern takes it — a document found it, or it was booked before the pattern was confirmed — is an OPEN QUESTION (`openQuestions()`, derived: link + private pattern + no `exception` mark), shown above the list and answered in the movement's window (`answer()`): «Ja» → the pattern is `mixed`, «Nein» → the mark `exception`. Booking a movement of a private pattern asks the same question IN the booking form (`booked()`; without an answer refused, `pattern-question`).
- **«Geschäftlich buchen …» (`Bank\BankPostingService::post()`, C2)** — one unit of work: the lines are built first by `GrossEntryBuilder` (the movement's amount is the GROSS, dated its booking date; a debit books «contra an Bank», a credit «Bank an contra», the bank side being the `ledger_account` of the movement's `BankAccount`); then a `BankPosting` row (table `bank_posting` — the OWNER), the link through `BankLinkService::link()` (owner type `posting`, key `posting:<id>`, basis `manual`; it locks the movement row and refuses a linked movement), the pattern (`booked()`), and the GENERATED entry through `LedgerService::post()` — source type `bank`, source ref `<bank account code>/<movement id> · <archived statement file>`, idempotency key `bank-posting:<id>` (one per posting row, so a movement taken back posts anew). The contra is an account the office names, or one of two NAMED ones from the config: «Privat» (Privatbezug / Privateinlage) and «Übertrag eigenes Konto» (the transfer clearing account) — both always without a tax code. The mandator's VAT liability decides whether a tax code is accepted at all.
- **The settings** — financialConfig `bankPosting`: `voucherFromGross` (`'100.00'`), read in ONE place (`BankPostingService::voucherFromGross()`); the two named accounts come from the MANDATOR record since C4 (`BankPostingService::namedAccount()` → `LedgerService::bankPostingAccount()`, the point «Privat and the transfer account» above). An account that is not set, or not in the chart, is refused when used, with a German sentence naming the mandator (BANK-POST-002). There is NO fallback in code (Rule 2): where a project override replaces financialConfig without the key (BOOT-CONFIG-001 in `bootstrap.md`), a posting with a tax code and without a file is refused naming the missing setting, and the help names no amount.
- **The attachment (decision 15) and where it lies** — with a tax code and a gross ≥ `voucherFromGross` a file must come with the posting, else nothing is saved; below it, and without a tax code, it is voluntary. Without a tax code the archived camt.053 is the voucher (the entry's origin names it; the window offers it as a download). The file (PDF / JPEG / PNG by a server-side sniff, ≤ 10 MB) goes into `Bank\BankVoucherArchive` — `data/archive/financial/bank-voucher/<YYYY>/<YYYYMMDD>-<bank account code>-<movement id>-<sha256, 16 hex>.<ext>` on the shared `WriteOnceStore`, inside the posting's unit of work; `bank_posting` names path, SHA-256 and the original name. DECIDED WITH C2 (the open point of ADR-061's consequences): NOT the DMS — financial keeps no dependency on a document module (ADR-060 decision 7), and a voucher needs none of a document store's functions, only «written once, never changed, found from its entry»; that is the module-agnostic block the two bank archives already use (Rule 9).
- **«Buchung zurücknehmen …» (`BankPostingService::takeBack()`, decision 19)** — `LedgerService::reverse()` on the entry's OWN date (the change of the bank's ledger account in that period nets to zero, which the check of C4 compares), the posting row keeps the reversal (who / when), the link is released in the same unit of work (decision 10), the movement is held on «zu prüfen» (`held()`). The ledger's period rules decide (decision 21): a closed year refuses, a VAT-settled period refuses an entry with a tax line — German sentences naming the manual correction; an entry without a tax line can still be taken back in a settled period, as every posting. A link of another module is not released here.
- **The screen since C2** — the list: selection checkbox (not on a business row) · state icon (opens the movement's WINDOW, `edit?id=`) · … · «Stand» · «Zuordnung» · «Saldo»; the toolbar's four views «Alle» · «Zu prüfen (n)» · «Geschäftlich» · «Privat» (`?state=`); above the list the open questions and the selection bar «Privat …» (`confirm-private`, a window naming the ticked rows). The window (`edit.tpl.php`, one POST with the hidden `op`): `book` · `private` · `review` · `lift` · `answer` · `take-back`; several forms, one action row (sibling forms, `form=`). `voucher?id=` shows the archived file of a posting, `statement?id=` downloads the movement's camt.053. Labels only — every explanation in `edit.help` / `list.help`.
- **Bank-driven bookkeeping** (ADR-061, owner 2026-11-17; C1–C5 built) — the model:
  - **Switch + date:** «Kontoauszug einlesen» plus «Abgleich ab» per bank account; no modes. Before the date movements are information only; switching off works forward only, links stay.
  - **Three states:** «geschäftlich» (linked to an invoice / open item / receipt, or booked via «Geschäftlich buchen …»), «privat» (a confirmed private pattern, or by hand), «zu prüfen» (everything else). Nothing is private by silence.
  - **Learned patterns** (counterparty / IBAN, merchant text, direction, approximate amount, day of month): one confirmation as private classifies the pattern; a document beats a pattern; booking a private-pattern movement as business asks, «Ja» makes the pattern «gemischt».
  - **The link** ties a movement to its owner and stores an amount (1:1 for now). Identity links automatically (creditor: QR reference; debtor: the entry `AcctSvcrRef`), likeness is proposed, without a unique candidate nothing is guessed.
  - **«Geschäftlich buchen …»** posts from a movement by an explicit act (contra account, optional VAT code; a file required with a VAT code from CHF 100, config). «Buchung zurücknehmen …» = reversing entry, link released, back to «zu prüfen».
  - **Mixed account:** private movements are never booked; the opening difference bank ↔ ledger is confirmed once as the ANCHOR, the check compares changes (ledger change = business movements). Transfers: mixed side «privat», business side against «Privat», two business accounts against the transfer clearing account (the mandator's; the ADR writes 1090, the KMU number).
  - **Close** (VAT return, year end) BLOCKS on: not read to the period end, «zu prüfen» in the period, check difference, the transfer account ≠ 0; WARNS on «Vorsteuer ohne Beleg».
- **The LUKB camt.054 ↔ camt.053 pair** (real files, counts only, 2026-11-17): incoming QR payments are COLLECTIVE credits in the camt.053 (PMNT / RCDT / VCOM, no `TxDtls`, no QR reference, 1–4 transactions); `Ntry/AcctSvcrRef` is the same entry reference in both files (every camt.054 entry found its camt.053 entry: amount, booking date, `NbOfTxs` equal); `Ntry/NtryRef` is a constant. Other credits come one per entry with `TxDtls`.
- **Payment methods hang on a bank account** (owner decision 15): module-receipt's `PaymentMethod` has `bank_account_code`; `PaymentMethodService::find()` / `all()` / `active()` resolve the bank account's ledger account on every read, `getCreditAccount()` answers it, and `save()` clears the method's own account. A payment method without a bank account (cash) keeps its own. Existing payment methods carry no bank account and behave as before.
- **The screens** (2026-11-17; traits in `src/Ui/`, thin hosts in module-backend `Finance/`, role `admin`): **Stammdaten › Finanzen › Bankkonten** (`BankAccountControllerTrait`, `/backend/finance/bank-account/…`) — a standard list (Bezeichnung, IBAN, Konto, Kontoauszug «nein» / «ja» / «bis <date>», «Aktiv»), action cell «+ Bankkonto», the window with Bezeichnung, Kürzel (read-only after creation), IBAN (read-only once a statement was read), Konto, Währung (shown, never posted) and the switch «Kontoauszug einlesen»; no delete. **Finanzen › Bankkonto** (`BankMovementControllerTrait`, `/backend/finance/bank-movement/…`) — the action cell IS the upload (the kernel `partials/upload` in its `cell` shape, XML or ZIP, the work area is the drop target; a CLICK opens the window `upload-info` first — one row per bank account that takes statements: Bankkonto, IBAN, «Eingelesen bis», «Laden ab» = the last stored day, and «Dateien wählen …», `UploadPolicy::$before`, owner 2026-11-18; dropped files are read at once), `upload` answers per file with `BankImportResult::message()` or the refusal sentence (fetch mode: the JSON envelope and the list replaced in place through `[data-bank-movements]`; page mode: flash + list; a ZIP: every XML on its own, one answer naming each), and the ONE list «Bewegungen» (`BankMovementListing`: Datum, Text, Betrag, Original, Saldo; searched in the database by date, text — bank text, counterparty, reference — and amount — booked OR original; header «eingelesen bis … · Saldo …»; no tabs, no row window). With more than one account that takes statements the choice stands at the top of the rail (`partials/bankAccountSelect`, `?account=<code>`); with none the page is one sentence and the action cell empty.
- **Screens carry labels only** (owner 2026-11-17, backend-screen.md §5): no explanatory sentence under a field, the switch or a list. What «Kontoauszug einlesen» means — and that every movement is stored, private ones too — stands in `edit.help` (`data-help-field="statement_import"`); the seamless rule and the search stand in the list help of «Bankkonto» (`f_date`, `f_text`, `f_amount`).
- **The contradiction hint** (decision 3): `Bank\BankAccountHints` collects classes the modules declare under the config key `bankAccountHints` (`BankAccountHintInterface`); financial names none. Debtor declares `PaymentTargetBankAccountHint` — an ACTIVE payment target with the same IBAN (plain or QR-IBAN) and another ledger account gives ONE short line in the bank account window, nowhere else.
- **What the real file of the stage-1 account showed** (read locally, structure only): camt.053.001.08; ONE statement per export over a free period; `OPBD` / `CLBD` / `CLAV`; `AcctSvcrRef` on every entry; one transaction per entry; QR-bill payments carry their QRR reference and the creditor's IBAN; card and TWINT purchases name the merchant only in `AddtlNtryInf`; a foreign purchase carries `InstdAmt` and `CcyXchg/XchgRate` as fields; booking date ≠ value date on two thirds of the entries.

## rules

- When a camt.053 file arrives (any screen, script or job) → MUST go through `BankStatementService::import()`; MUST NOT write `bank_statement` / `bank_movement` rows any other way — the import owns the chain, the dedup and the archive.
- When changing the import → MUST keep the chain seamless: a statement that does not overlap the last stored day (a gap, and a start the day after) and an overlap that does not agree are REFUSED with a German sentence naming the dates and balances — the only statement spared the overlap is one following another of the same account in the same file; MUST NOT accept a date gap by a weekend / holiday rule, by equal balances or by a sequence number; MUST NOT store a statement «with a note», and MUST NOT insert a movement into the covered period or in front of its start.
- When deciding which entries of a file are new → MUST go by the bank's entry reference (the dedup key) and prove the join by the balance; MUST NOT cut by comparing booking dates with the covered end (movements booked on that day after an earlier export would be lost).
- When code needs the ledger account of a bank → MUST read it from `BankAccount` (through `BankAccountService` or, for a payment method, `PaymentMethodService`); MUST NOT store a copy of it next to an IBAN or on a payment method (Rule 2).
- When reading payment methods for their credit account → MUST go through `PaymentMethodService` (`find()`, `all()`, `active()`); MUST NOT read `PaymentMethod` straight from its repository — a bank-bound method answers an empty credit account there.
- When a movement would lead to a ledger entry → MUST post either through the owning module's document or through «Geschäftlich buchen …» by an explicit act of the office (ADR-061 decision 14); MUST NOT post from a movement automatically.
- When building states, links, patterns or the close check (stages C1–C5) → MUST follow ADR-061; MUST NOT reintroduce modes, a second switch or a silent default «privat» — a movement without link or pattern is «zu prüfen».
- When linking a debtor payment to a camt.053 movement → MUST link by the ENTRY reference (`Ntry/AcctSvcrRef` of the camt.054 entry = that of the camt.053 entry); MUST NOT use `Ntry/NtryRef` (a constant per QR-IBAN and participant number) or the transaction-level reference.
- When a link is set without the office confirming → MUST be an identity (creditor: QR reference identical; debtor: entry `AcctSvcrRef`); everything else MUST be a proposal, and MUST NOT be guessed when no candidate is unique.
- When a module links a movement to one of its records → MUST call `BankLinkService::link()` inside its OWN unit of work, its own row locked first, and release through `BankLinkService::release()` in the same unit of work as its own correction (ADR-061 decision 10); MUST NOT write `bank_link` itself, and financial MUST NOT resolve an owner key or name a consumer (Rule 9).
- When offering movements to an owner → MUST read them through `BankLinkService::freeMovements()` / `freeMovement()`; MUST NOT offer a linked movement, a reversal, or a movement of a bank account that is inactive or whose «Kontoauszug einlesen» is off.
- When storing a link → MUST store an amount (rule for now: one link, the full amount); MUST NOT model it as a yes / no flag on the movement.
- When a foreign amount is taken from a movement → MUST take the movement's CHF and the structured `CcyXchg/XchgRate` (the effective rate, surcharge included); MUST NOT match against or show the «Wechselkurs» written in the entry text.
- When matching a card payment to a creditor → MUST use the merchant text (creditor field «Banktext») or original amount + currency + date; MUST NOT expect a counterparty name (cards carry none).
- When a learned pattern classifies movements → MUST let a document match win over a private pattern, and MUST ask before a private pattern turns «gemischt» (ADR-061 decisions 7, 8).
- When a module's movement must never be private (it is that module's to link) → MUST declare a `BankMovementClaimInterface` under `bankMovementClaims`; MUST NOT let financial recognise it by a bank code, a text or a module name itself (Rule 9), and MUST NOT guard «Privat» in a template or in JavaScript only — `BankMovementStates::markPrivate()` refuses.
- When the upload meets a statement of an account that is no bank account here → MUST skip it and name it in the one neutral line; MUST NOT drop it silently and MUST NOT answer an error sentence per account.
- When code needs the state of a movement → MUST read it through `BankMovementRepository` (`STATE`, `statesOf()`, `stateCounts()`, `search($state)`) or `BankMovementStates::stateOf()`; MUST NOT store a state on the movement or derive it a second time elsewhere — a link, a mark and a pattern are the only facts.
- When the key rule of a pattern changes (`BankPattern::keyOf()`) → MUST ship a migration that refills `bank_movement.pattern_key` and rewrites `bank_pattern.match_key` consistently; MUST NOT edit the frozen copy in `Version20261117170000`.
- When a movement is posted directly → MUST go through `BankPostingService::post()` (the `BankPosting` owner row, the link through `BankLinkService`, the generated entry through `LedgerService::post()` with source `bank`); MUST NOT write a manual journal entry for it or a `bank_link` row by hand, and MUST NOT post without the office's explicit act.
- When a direct posting is wrong → MUST take it back through `BankPostingService::takeBack()` (reversing entry, link released, movement held on «zu prüfen» in one unit of work); MUST NOT retract or amend the entry, delete the `bank_posting` row or its voucher.
- When the attachment threshold is needed → MUST read financialConfig `bankPosting` through `BankPostingService::voucherFromGross()`; MUST NOT write an amount into code or a template.
- When a file is attached to a posting → MUST store it through `BankVoucherArchive` inside the posting's unit of work; MUST NOT add a delete or replace path, and MUST NOT make financial depend on a document module for it.
- When creditor «Bezahlt am» is set on a bank account with «Kontoauszug einlesen» on, dated from «Abgleich ab» → MUST take it and the CHF amount from a movement; MUST NOT accept them typed (ADR-061 decision 13).
- When a period with a switched-on bank account is closed → MUST block on «zu prüfen» movements, a statement not read to the period end, a check difference and a transfer account ≠ 0; MUST NOT downgrade these to warnings.
- When code asks whether a bank account is bank-driven → MUST ask `BankAccount::isBankDriven()` / `drives($day)` (in SQL `BankMovementRepository::DRIVEN`); MUST NOT read the switch alone, add a mode or a second switch, or store «driven» on a movement — switching off and moving the date work only because nothing is stored.
- When a decision about a movement is added (a mark, a posting, a new action) → MUST refuse it for a movement that is information only (`BankRefusedException::notDriven()`); MUST NOT give such a movement a state, a checkbox or a place in the check.
- When the check, the anchor or a close figure is needed → MUST go through `Bank\BankReconciliation` (`check()`, `anchor()`, `confirm()`, `transferBalance()`, `taxWithoutVoucher()`); MUST NOT compute a bank ↔ ledger difference elsewhere, and MUST NOT store a check result — only the confirmed anchor is a row.
- When a module posts for a record it links to a bank movement → MUST declare a `BankEntryOwnerInterface` under `bankEntryOwners` that answers the link's owner key for its entries; MUST NOT let financial resolve an owner key or a source reference itself (Rule 9) — without the answer the module's postings show as «Buchung ohne Bewegung».
- When the «Privat» or the transfer clearing account is needed → MUST read it through `LedgerService::bankPostingAccount()` (the mandator's `private` / `bank-transfer`); MUST NOT write an account number into code, a template or a config default — no number is assumed, 1090 least of all.
- When the check compares the ledger account → MUST compare CHANGES with the year opening left out; MUST NOT compare absolute balances (a mixed account's differ by the private history — that is the anchor's job).
- When parsing any camt file → MUST use `Z77\Shared\Payment\Camt\CamtParser`; MUST NOT open the XML with an own SimpleXML / DOM call in a module (one parser, hardened once).
- When writing a test, a fixture, a doc or a log line → MUST use synthetic bank data; MUST NOT copy names, IBANs, references, texts or amounts from a real statement into the repository, a log or an error mail.
- When a bank account or a statement is in another currency than the base currency → MUST refuse with the German sentence; MUST NOT convert or store it (ADR-060 decision 8).
- When changing `BankStatementArchive` → MUST NOT add a delete or overwrite path; an archived statement file is a booking record kept ten years.
- When changing a bank screen → MUST escape every movement value on output (bank text is foreign input) and keep the screen to labels, answers and refusals; MUST NOT add explanatory text to the screen (it goes into the help, backend-screen.md §5) and MUST NOT put matching or refusal logic into JavaScript (Rule 7).
- When another module holds a ledger account for an IBAN of its own → MUST report a contradiction through `bankAccountHints` (`BankAccountHintInterface`); MUST NOT let financial read that module, and MUST NOT show the hint anywhere but in the bank account window.

- **BANK-SCREEN-001** — resolved 2026-11-17: the three screens are built (boards approved by the owner the same day). Don't assume the owner has seen them in the browser yet — see `## pending`.
- **BANK-ACL-001** — don't assume a separate access right «Bankkonten» exists: the backend knows only the role ladder (`editor` < `admin` < `superUser`). - **BANK-START-001** — don't assume history can be added in front: the first statement of an account sets the start of its chain, and a file bringing earlier movements is refused (`before-start`). Choosing an earlier start means a new beginning for that account — not built. Pick the first file with that in mind (e.g. from 1 January of the first year wanted).
- **BANK-BATCH-001** — don't assume a collective entry is split: a statement entry with several transactions is ONE movement with the entry's amount, without counterparty and reference. The mixed account's real file has none; the account customer payments arrive on books incoming QR payments as collective credits (1–4 transactions, often without `TxDtls`) — only the camt.054 names the payments (ADR-061 decision 12).

The stage-1 bank sends a reference on every entry, so this path is a fallback.
- **BANK-UPLOAD-001** — don't assume several files dropped at once are read oldest first: the upload component sends one request per file in the order the browser hands them over. A later file read before an earlier one is refused (`no-overlap`) and names the date — upload the oldest first (the help says so).
- **BANK-OVERLAP-001** — don't assume every bank can be read: since 2026-11-18 a file must OVERLAP the last stored day. A bank whose e-banking only hands out FIXED periodic statements (January to 31.01., February from 01.02.) with no free date range never overlaps — such an account cannot be continued after its first file (owner decision 2026-11-18, accepted as a known limit; the stage-1 bank offers a free range). Per-day statements are read when they come in ONE file (the exception inside a file); as separate XML files of a ZIP each one is judged on its own and the second is refused.
- **BANK-OVERLAP-002** — don't assume several history files can be loaded back to back: quarter files 01.01.–31.03. and 01.04.–30.06. no longer join. Download them overlapping (the second from 31.03.), or the history as one file.
- **BANK-CLAIM-001** — don't assume every collective credit is guarded against «Privat»: debtor's claim recognises the bank transaction code `PMNT/RCDT/VCOM` on a bank account whose IBAN an ACTIVE payment target carries. A bank that codes its QR collective credits otherwise, or a payment target without that IBAN, is not recognised — «Privat» then works as before. A pattern learned BEFORE 2026-11-18 stays: lift it in a movement's window («Muster aufheben») or answer the open question.
- **BANK-SKIP-001** — don't assume a skipped account is a decision that is remembered: every upload names the accounts that are no bank account again (there is no «ignore this IBAN» list — not asked for). - **BANK-SEQ-001** — don't assume the statement sequence number is checked: it is stored only. SIX's implementation guideline and the stage-1 bank's handbook define `ElctrncSeqNb` as the running number per account and year, but the bank's e-banking downloads do not follow it (observed 2026-11-18: 1 and 2 on one account, 1 twice on another) — not usable. The chain is guarded by the overlap and the balances.
- **BANK-PAGE-001** — don't assume a paginated statement (`GrpHdr/MsgPgntn` with several pages, intermediate balances) was ever read: each page is judged as its own statement if it carries `OPBD` and `CLBD`; not verified against a real paginated file.
- **BANK-LINK-001** — don't assume a link can be partial or shared: stage C1 allows ONE link per movement over its full amount and one movement per owner (`uniq_bank_link_movement`, `uniq_bank_link_owner`, `link-amount`). - **BANK-LINK-002** — don't assume the import links anything: `BankStatementService::import()` knows no owner (the upload stays cheap). Creditor's job `creditor-intake` links the reference identities on its next pass (within a minute on the shipped schedule); with that schedule switched off only «Zahlungen suchen …» in the Kreditorenjournal does. - **BANK-LINK-004** — resolved 2026-11-17 (stage C2): the view «Ohne Zuordnung» is gone; the work list is «Zu prüfen», and it empties — a movement leaves it by a link, a posting or «Privat».
- **BANK-STATE-001** — resolved 2026-11-17 (stage C4): the states apply only from «Abgleich ab» on an account with «Kontoauszug einlesen» on. Don't assume a bank account read before C4 shows states after the deploy: its date is empty until the office sets it (Stammdaten › Finanzen › Bankkonten) — until then its movements are information only.
- **BANK-CHECK-001** — don't assume «Abgleich ab» may lie before the first statement: the chain cannot be extended in front (BANK-START-001), so the check then blocks («der Kontoauszug beginnt erst am …») and the anchor is `unread`. Set the date to the first day the statement covers.
- **BANK-CHECK-002** — don't assume the check balances per PERIOD for an account customer payments arrive on: debtor posts a payment on the payment's own date (`PaymentPostingBuilder`), the bank books the collective credit on its booking date. Where the two fall into different periods, both periods fail honestly (the posting «ohne Bewegung» with the note «Bewegung am …», the movement «ohne Buchung») and BLOCK their close; over both periods the check passes. Debtor's switch to the booking date (ADR-060 decision 11) is not built — an open owner decision. The same holds for a creditor invoice posted with a TYPED date that was linked afterwards (retro, decision 2) to a movement booked in another period.
- **BANK-CHECK-003** — resolved 2026-11-17 (C4 + C5 merged): receipt declares `ReceiptBankEntryOwner`; a posted, linked receipt is joined to its movement. Don't assume more: a receipt is posted on its RECEIPT date and the bank books the card payment days later — across a period end both periods fail (BANK-CHECK-002, the third case beside debtor and the retro-linked creditor invoice); and a receipt posted onto a bank-driven account's ledger account WITHOUT a link (nobody ran «Zahlungen suchen …» in the Belegjournal) is a «Buchung ohne Bewegung» while its card movement stays «zu prüfen» — both block the close until the office links them.
- **BANK-CHECK-004** — don't assume an amount difference inside a matched pair is named on its own: the check names the total difference and the postings without a movement; an entry whose amount on the ledger account differs from its movement's (not possible through `link()` today — one link, the full amount) would show in the difference only.
- **BANK-CHECK-005** — don't assume the window «Abgleich …» was seen in a browser: built without a board (owner: «pull it through»); the year tabs inside the window open the window again through `data-window-open` — checked in the harness only.
- **BANK-CLOSE-001** — don't assume the block of the VAT return is live: the check is registered under `vat-return` like creditor's, receipt's and debtor's, but the VAT return itself is not built (financial.md «Not built yet») — nothing asks that scope today except the harness. The year close (`period-close`) asks it now; a month close does not exist.
- **BANK-ANCHOR-001** — don't assume an unconfirmed anchor blocks a close: ADR-061 decision 25 lists four blocks, the anchor is not one. It is a WARNING at the year close and visible in «Abgleich …». The check itself does not need it (it compares changes).
- **BANK-STATE-002** — don't assume a «gemischt» pattern can become private again: there is no way back in the screen (its movements are decided one by one). `liftPattern()` works on a private pattern only.
- **BANK-STATE-003** — don't assume a pattern key survives a bank's change of wording: the key is the IBAN where the bank sends one; for card and TWINT purchases it is the text after «Bezugsort:» up to the comma, checked against SYNTHETIC texts only (the plan's risk «pattern keys on real bank texts»). A merchant written differently is a new key — «zu prüfen», never silently private.
- **BANK-STATE-004** — don't assume one key can hold two private narrowings: there is ONE pattern per bank account, direction and key; confirming again with another narrowing replaces nothing — the second movement is marked by hand.
- **BANK-STATE-005** — don't assume a reversal (`RvslInd`) is special for the states: it is «zu prüfen» like every movement and CAN be posted directly or set private (it cannot be linked by an owner — BANK-LINK-005). What a returned payment should do is undecided (ADR-061 consequences).
- **BANK-POST-001** — don't assume a movement can be posted in parts or to several accounts: «Geschäftlich buchen …» takes the full amount against ONE contra account with at most one tax code (the 1:1 link, decision 9). A partly business movement is undecided (ADR-061 consequences) — today: post it whole and correct by hand, or mark it private.
- **BANK-POST-002** — don't assume «Privat» and «Übertrag eigenes Konto» work out of the box: their accounts stand on the mandator record (`private`, `bank-transfer`, since C4) and are EMPTY until the office types them; the shipped KMU chart (legal-entity variant, FIN-CHART-002) holds no such account — the choice is refused with a sentence naming the mandator until both the account and the setting exist.
- **BANK-POST-003** — don't assume a voucher can be replaced or added later: it is attached when the posting is made and archived write-once. A wrong file = «Buchung zurücknehmen» and book again; a voluntary voucher forgotten stays missing.
- **BANK-POST-004** — resolved 2026-11-17 (stage C4): the control line «Vorsteuer ohne Beleg» is a warning at the VAT return and a line in «Abgleich …».
- **BANK-POST-005** — don't assume the two sides of a transfer are PAIRED: each side is its own posting against the transfer clearing account; since C4 its balance ≠ 0 at a period end blocks the close, which shows a missing side — not which one.
- **BANK-ARCHIVE-001** — don't assume the lost-race guard was reachable before: the import locks the bank account rows before it writes, so the loser of two imports of one file finds the winner's file in place and never wrote it itself. The guard (keep the file when a committed row names it) is defensive and tested through the service's own cleanup step.
- **BANK-LINK-005** — don't assume a reversal (`RvslInd`) can be linked: it is never free (undecided, ADR-061 consequences).
- **BANK-CONTRA-001** — resolved 2026-11-17: the hint is built (`bankAccountHints`, debtor's `PaymentTargetBankAccountHint`), in the bank account window only. Don't assume debtor reads its payment target's account from «Bankkonten» — that comes later, by a new owner decision.
