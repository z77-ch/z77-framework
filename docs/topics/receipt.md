## mental model

A phone captures a receipt — photo, gross amount, optionally a text, a TEMPLATE (what was bought: debit account and VAT code) and a PAYMENT METHOD («Bezahlt mit»: the credit account) — into the RECEIPT JOURNAL; the ledger sees nothing. The owner reviews, corrects and splits receipts in the backend and posts every receipt up to a CUT-OFF date in one go, «Journal verbuchen»: one generated journal entry per receipt, one unit of work, all or nothing — the flow of debtor's Faktura-Journal (ADR-054), built on its pattern, not its classes. Owner decisions E1–E12 and the approved proposals T2 / T4 of 2026-11-12 (`gateway-app-kanal-bauplan.md`) are the source.

- **Owner decisions after the plan (2026-11-12):** **E10** — the credit account is NOT part of the template; a second master-data entity, the payment method («Bezahlt mit»: Kasse, Firmenkarte, privat ausgelegt), carries it. **E11** — «Journal verbuchen» takes a cut-off date; the run is every captured receipt dated on or before it, an incomplete one blocks the whole run, later receipts stay untouched. **E16** — «Journal verbuchen» is a WINDOW, the cut-off PROPOSED = TODAY (replaces E11's «last day of the previous month», which made the window look empty), changeable; errors up to it → only the errors, else a short summary and «Jetzt buchen»; the Faktura-Journal gets the same window without a date. **E12** — the phone captures ONE VAT code per receipt; a rare mixed-VAT receipt is SPLIT in the journal into two receipts sharing the photo.
- **Module direction:** module-receipt requires financial (accounts, VAT, posting), dms (the photo), vat, mandator; module-financial gains neither dms nor api (T4). The API targets and the app screen are DECLARED in receiptConfig (`apiServices`, `apiDeviceScopes`, `appScreens`) and found by module-api — this package does not name it (harness O2).
- **Master data is file-based** — `ReceiptTemplate` (`data/framework/receipt/receipt_templates.json`: code, label, icon, debit account, tax code — '' = without VAT, position, active) and `PaymentMethod` (`payment_methods.json`: code, label, credit account, position, active). The pattern of the order status and the payment terms that E3 names: a handful of rows per installation, edited in the backend, referenced BY `code` (ADR-043 decision 19: `receipt.template_code`, `receipt.payment_method_code`), deactivated never deleted, code immutable (`ReceiptTemplateService`, `PaymentMethodService`). Not seeded: the accounts depend on the chart. Every save checks the accounts and the tax code against the chart and module-vat.
- **A payment method may hang on a bank account** (owner decision 15 of 2026-11-16, ADR-060 decision 9): `PaymentMethod::$bankAccountCode` names a bank account of module-financial's master data «Bankkonten» ([`bank.md`](bank.md)); its credit account is then that bank account's ledger account — resolved on EVERY read by `PaymentMethodService` (`find()`, `all()`, `active()`), never copied: the row stores `bank_account_code` and an empty `credit_account` (`save()` clears a typed one), and a change of the ledger account in «Bankkonten» reaches every payment method at once. Without a bank account (cash, «privat ausgelegt») the method keeps its own `credit_account` — every payment method that existed before carries none and behaves as before. `getCreditAccount()` answers either; `getOwnCreditAccount()` is the stored one. The validator requires the bank account to exist and be active, and an own account only without one. The payment-method window has the field «Bankkonto» since 2026-11-17 (a select: «— keines (Kasse, privat) —» — so labelled since 2026-11-20, owner — and the active bank accounts; the help's general part says that the cash box is a payment method WITHOUT bank account and Haben `1000` Kasse, never a «Bankkonto», which requires an IBAN); with one chosen «Haben» shows the bank account's ledger account read-only, and the help explains it (`data-help-field="bank_account_code"`) — no text on the screen (backend-screen.md §5). Verified: `B7`. Verified: `tests/module-receipt.php` `B1`–`B6`.
- **The receipt row** (`receipt`, Doctrine): date, text, GROSS amount (base currency), `template_code`, `payment_method_code`, `debit_account` / `credit_account` (numbers — the payment method's credit account RESOLVED at capture, correctable), `tax_code` OR `without_vat`, `document_id` (DMS, no FK, indexed — split parts share it), `device_id` / `device_name` / `captured_by` (snapshots, E8), `capture_key` (unique), `changed_by/at`, state `captured` → `posted`, `ledger_entry_ref` (`{year}/{number}`, debtor's shape), `journal_posting_id`, `posted_by/at`, `version` (`ORM\Version`).
- **Complete** = debit, credit, the VAT decision (a tax code or «ohne MWST») and a text (`Receipt::missing()` lists what lacks, in German). The text is part of it because the ledger refuses an entry without one.
- **Capture** (`ReceiptCaptureService::capture()`) needs no session — who captured it is data from the device principal. Date default today, never future (E6); amount required, positive (E5); text default = template label; an unknown template or payment method refused, a deactivated one still applied (an offline queue may send late). A known `capture_key` returns the existing receipt and stores nothing (E7).
- **The photo** goes through the DMS's trusted system path (T2): `DocumentService::forModule('receipt', 'Belege')` → `ModuleDrive::folder(<fiscal-year code>, <month>)` → `saveGenerated()` — `Belege/2026/11` (E4; the calendar year when no fiscal year covers the date). Because that path does not sniff, the capture does: `finfo_buffer` on the BYTES, only `image/jpeg`, `image/png`, `application/pdf`, at most 10 MiB (`ReceiptCaptureService::MAX_PHOTO_BYTES`); the stored file name gets the extension of the sniffed type.
- **Correction, split, deletion** (`ReceiptJournalService`): with the version the caller showed; row locked FOR UPDATE, compared, read fresh. Values checked by the same `ReceiptFields` as the capture. `split($id, $version, $amountOfNewPart)` (E12) reduces the receipt and creates a second captured receipt with the part — same photo, date, text, payment method and credit account, capture data; debit and VAT left open for the owner; no capture key; the two amounts sum to the original. Deleting returns the photo's document id only when no other receipt names it (null while a split part still does); the photo then goes to the DMS trash (see «Löschen …» and the photo).
- **The posting** (`ReceiptPostingBuilder`): a receipt is ONE gross part (Soll = the template's account) against the payment method's account, so financial's public `GrossEntryBuilder` builds the lines — the same split as the one-line entry (VAT in gross mode at the rate of the receipt date, net on the P&L side, the tax line on the mandator's VAT account, zero / exempt without tax line; since 2026-11-15, RECEIPT-BUILD-001; before, the UI class `OneLineEntryForm` was driven as a builder) — and `ReceiptPostingBuilder` wraps them as `PostingRequest::generated(date, text, 'receipt', <id>, 'receipt:<id>', lines)`.
- **«Journal verbuchen»** (`ReceiptJournalPostingService`, E11): `preview($cutOff)` returns the run (`seen`: every captured receipt dated ≤ cut-off with its version), what blocks it, `postable`, the rows per account; `post($cutOff, $seen)` locks the rows EXCLUSIVE by id, refuses `changed` when a version differs or the run is no longer exactly the captured receipts ≤ cut-off, refuses `incomplete` when any receipt of the run lacks something, then writes a protocol row (`receipt_journal_posting`: who, when, count, gross) and posts per receipt in date order through `LedgerService::post()`. Lock order: receipt rows → `journal-entry.*`. Any ledger refusal (no fiscal year, closed or VAT-settled period, account refused) rolls the whole run back; the refusal comes out German, naming the receipt.
- **The phone (wave 2, part E, 2026-11-12):** three targets on module-api's gateway, all scope `receipt.capture`, all refusing a machine (bearer) caller with 403 (`Api\DeviceOnlyTarget` — the gateway lets a bearer key past the scope check by design). `GET receipt-templates` → `{"templates": [{code, label, icon, position}]}` (active, tile order); `GET payment-methods` → `{"payment_methods": [{code, label}]}` — no account, no VAT code on the wire (E2, E10), ETag = payload hash. `POST receipts` (multipart: `photo` + `amount`, `date`, `text`, `template`, `payment_method`) → `capture()` with the owner's name as `capturedBy`, the device id and LABEL (`ApiPrincipal::$deviceLabel`, E8), the request's `Idempotency-Key` as capture key (E7); answers `{"receipt": {id, date, amount, text, template, payment_method, state}, "message": "Beleg erfasst"}` (the journal position, never a ledger number, E1) or 422 `invalid_fields` with `InvalidReceiptException::$errors` in `error.fields`; an upload PHP dropped says «zu gross» / «nicht vollständig angekommen» instead of «Das Foto fehlt.». The scope is offered to app users by `apiDeviceScopes` (label «Belege erfassen») with the MINIMUM ROLE `'realms' => ['backend' => AuthRole::EDITOR]` (ADR-058, review B7): the linked backend user must be EDITOR or above — when it is assigned AND on every request (a role taken away stops capture at once); no `member` key, so never for members. `capturedBy` is the linked person's name (`ApiPrincipal::$ownerName`); the device belongs to the app user it was signed in as (api.md «app users»).
- **The app screen** (`appScreens` → `res/view/templates/App/receipt.tpl.php` + `res/assets/js/app/receipt.js`, inside module-api's shell `/app`, [`api.md`](api.md) «app shell»): tiles (the templates + «Ohne Vorlage»; a tap asks the server FIRST — `shell.allows('receipt.capture')`, owner 2026-11-13 — and opens the camera only when allowed: refused → «Dein App-Zugang darf keine Belege erfassen …», no camera; offline → the last known state; «Zugang wird geprüft …» after 400 ms; the input is clicked after the answer, so a browser that no longer counts it as the tap opens nothing and «Foto aufnehmen» is one more tap — to watch on the iPhone, API-008 (a)), capture (photo scaled on the phone to ≤ 1600 px JPEG on a canvas, Betrag, Datum = today, Text, the template chip, «Bezahlt mit» chips, the last choice preselected), confirmation «Beleg erfasst» / «Beleg gespeichert» (offline), «Heute gesendet». The tile symbols: keys `ReceiptTemplate::ICONS`, glyphs `ReceiptIcons::GLYPHS` (harness O21: every offered key has one; unknown → `receipt`).

- **Retention of a posted receipt's photo** (owner decision 2026-11-15; Swiss OR 958f, MWSTG Art. 70 — business records 10 years from the end of the business year): `ReceiptRetention::apply()` gives the photo of every POSTED receipt the DMS `retentionUntil` = the END of the fiscal year (module-financial `FiscalYear`) covering the RECEIPT DATE — the date of its journal entry, not `posted_at` — plus 10 years (`ReceiptRetention::YEARS`), e.g. FY 2026 → `2036-12-31`; without a fiscal year (a posted receipt always had one — only a deleted year leaves none) 31.12. of the calendar year + 10; a year ending 29.02. ends 28.02. Split parts sharing a photo: the latest date wins. Written through module-dms `ModuleDrive::retainUntil()` (server-side, only extends, idempotent, one flush). From then the DMS refuses the purge (also «Papierkorb leeren», which skips it and says «übersprungen (Aufbewahrungsfrist)») and an overwrite; a soft-delete into the Drive's trash stays possible and restorable. A captured receipt's photo has no deadline. **Not in the unit of work:** the documents are a FILE store, the receipts DB rows — `post()` calls `apply()` AFTER the commit (a rolled-back run sets nothing); `apply()` covers every posted receipt, so it is also the BACKFILL: it runs after every «Journal verbuchen» and when the tab «Verbuchte Journale» opens (no Doctrine migration — it cannot reach the file store; the pattern of order's `OrderStatusUpgrade`).

The link is financial's ([`bank.md`](bank.md) «The link», port `BankLinkService`): owner type `receipt`, key `receipt:<id>` (`Receipt::bankOwnerKey()`), label «Beleg <id>» (`bankLabel()` — it never changes, nothing relabels). A linked movement is «geschäftlich» by financial's derived state; receipt stores nothing about it.
  - **Which bank account** (`ReceiptBankMatchService::bankAccountsOf()`): the bank account(s) whose `ledger_account` is the receipt's «Haben» — where the posting lands, whether the payment method put it there or the office typed it. The payment method's own `bank_account_code` decides between two bank accounts on one ledger account and stands in while «Haben» is empty. Cash and «privat ausgelegt» have none: never matched, never offered.
  - **The judgement** (`find()` → `ReceiptBankMatch`), over the FREE debit movements of that bank account, booking date from `daysBefore` before to `daysAfter` after the RECEIPT DATE (`receiptConfig['bankMatch']`, 2 / 10 — card bookings lag): the booked amount equal and exactly ONE movement — or several, of which exactly one carries a word (≥ 4 characters) of the receipt's text — and no other unlinked receipt of that amount could claim it → `proposal` (level 2, ticked in advance); else `candidates` (the named ones, then the nearest date). A receipt has no reference: there is NO identity level, nothing links without the office, no job.
  - **Foreign currency without receipt FX** (level 3): a CAPTURED receipt nothing fits in CHF, and a movement whose ORIGINAL amount in one of `ReceiptFields::foreignCurrencies()` equals the receipt's amount → `candidates`, never ticked (the receipt names no currency — the likeness is the number alone). Taken, `Receipt::amountFromBank()` sets the receipt's amount to the movement's CHF (the effective rate; the «Wechselkurs» of the entry text is never read). The original amount is NOT stored on the receipt (no column before the FX build, RECEIPT-BANK-002).
  - **The writes** (`ReceiptJournalService`): `takeMovement($id, $version, $movementId, $basis)` — one unit of work, the receipt row locked first (any state), then `BankLinkService::link()`; the server judges the pair again (free debit, the receipt's bank account, the amount — or the foreign exception above); a POSTED receipt is linked without a posting and without any change. `ReceiptBankMatchService::take()` judges the basis (`proposal` when the movement is the proposed one, else `manual`). `releaseMovement()` («Zuordnung lösen») — captured: the link is gone, the amount stays; posted: refused in German (the correction of a posted receipt is not built). Refusals: `ReceiptRefusedException::BANK`.
  - **While linked** amount and «Haben» are the bank's: `update()` refuses a change of either, `split()` refuses («Zuerst im Beleg «Zuordnung lösen»»); date, text, Soll and VAT stay correctable. `delete()` releases the link in the same unit of work (ADR-061 decision 10).
  - **Screens** (labels only; help sections `bank` in `list.help` and `edit.help`): toolbar tool «Zahlungen suchen …» (only while a bank account reads statements) → window `confirm-bank` (mask `receipt-bank`): a fieldset per unlinked receipt (captured AND posted, dated since the oldest free movement), one radio group `take[<id>]` = `<version>:<movement id>`, proposals ticked, «nicht zuordnen»; «Übernehmen» takes each pair in its own unit of work, a refused one is named in the flash. The receipt window: «Bank · date · amount» under the amount, amount / «Haben» / «Bezahlt mit» locked, «Zuordnung lösen» (`release-movement`); unlinked and bank-paid: «Zahlung suchen …» (`confirm-bank?id=`). The list marks a linked row «Bank» beside «Bezahlt mit». No board was drawn (Rule 8) — the screens mirror creditor's C1 ones.
  - **Duplicated from module-creditor on purpose** (Rule 9, the extraction is pending): `ReceiptBankMatch` ≈ `CreditorBankMatch`, `confirmBank.tpl.php` ≈ creditor's, `confirmBankAction()` / the take parser / `releaseMovementAction()` ≈ creditor's trait, and in the match service `cleanDays()`, `normal()`, `names()`, `nearest()`, `pending()`'s scan loop. Creditor was being edited by stage C4 in parallel.
  - Verified: `tests/module-receipt.php` `K1`–`K20`.
- **Foreign currencies — the list exists before receipt's own FX (2026-11-16, creditor stage A0):** the build plan [`receipt-foreign-currency-bauplan.md`](../03-development/receipt-foreign-currency-bauplan.md) (F1–F14, `[CONCEPT]`) is NOT built for receipts — a receipt is still CHF only. Built early because module-creditor needs it (owner decision 2026-11-15): `receiptConfig['foreignCurrencies']` (`['EUR', 'USD']` — F7 named only EUR; USD for the creditor invoices) and in `ReceiptFields` the T3 parts `foreignCurrencies()` (the ONE reader; an entry not `^[A-Z]{3}$`, the base currency or one of `NOT_TWO_DECIMALS` throws), `foreignCurrency($value, $field, &$errors, $kept)` (''/base → null; in the list or the document's own; else «Währung … ist nicht freigegeben (erlaubt: …).»), `originalAmount($value, $currency, …)` (required with a foreign currency, refused without one) and `derivedRate(Money $chf, Money $original)` (CHF per unit, 6 places, half away from zero — derived, never typed). Receipt's FX build reuses them (T3's `exchangeRate()` / `chfFromRate()` are still to come) — no second list. A project overriding `receiptConfig` without the key gets the shipped default (`ReceiptFields::DEFAULT_FOREIGN_CURRENCIES`, BOOT-CONFIG-001).

## rules

- When changing who may capture receipts from the phone → MUST change the minimum role in `apiDeviceScopes['receipt.capture']['realms']` (receiptConfig, ADR-058); MUST NOT check roles in the targets — the gateway filters the app user's scopes live
- When capturing a receipt from any channel (API target, backend upload, import) → MUST call `ReceiptCaptureService::capture()` with who captured it as data; MUST NOT write a `Receipt` directly, MUST NOT read the actor from an `AuthService` on the device path (stateless, T1)
- When accepting a receipt photo → MUST let `ReceiptCaptureService` sniff the BYTES and name the file from the sniffed type; MUST NOT trust the client's file name or MIME, and MUST NOT widen `PHOTO_TYPES` (e.g. HEIC) without an owner decision — the trusted `saveGenerated()` path does not check it again
- When storing a receipt photo → MUST go through `DocumentService::forModule(ReceiptCaptureService::MODULE_KEY, …)` → `ModuleDrive`; MUST NOT use a folder id outside the module partition
- When code needs a payment method's credit account → MUST read the method through `PaymentMethodService` (`find()`, `all()`, `active()`), which resolves a bank-bound method's account from «Bankkonten»; MUST NOT read `PaymentMethod` straight from its repository for that — it answers an empty account for a bank-bound method — and MUST NOT copy the bank account's ledger account into the payment method.
- When a credit account must be offered on the phone → MUST come from a `PaymentMethod` (E10); MUST NOT add a credit account to `ReceiptTemplate` or let the phone choose a free account (E2)
- When a receipt is posted → its photo MUST get the legal deadline through `ReceiptRetention::apply()` AFTER the posting's unit of work committed; MUST NOT write `Document::setRetentionUntil()` from this module, MUST NOT call `apply()` inside a Doctrine transaction (a file write does not roll back) and MUST NOT shorten or clear a deadline (the DMS handle only extends)
- When posting receipts → MUST call `ReceiptJournalPostingService::post($cutOff, $seen)` with the cut-off and the `seen` list `preview($cutOff)` returned; MUST NOT post a subset of the run, MUST NOT post a receipt through `LedgerService` elsewhere and MUST NOT use another source type or idempotency key than `receipt` / `receipt:{id}`
- When building a receipt's posting lines → MUST go through `ReceiptPostingBuilder` (financial's `GrossEntryBuilder`, liability not applied — `null`); MUST NOT compute VAT, net side or tax account in this module, and MUST NOT drive the UI form `OneLineEntryForm` again
- When a receipt carries two VAT rates → MUST split it with `ReceiptJournalService::split()` (E12); MUST NOT post it as a manual Sammelbuchung beside the journal or add a second VAT code to a receipt
- When changing, splitting or deleting a captured receipt → MUST pass the version the screen showed and handle `ReceiptRefusedException::CHANGED` by reloading; MUST NOT write a posted receipt (the entity refuses)
- When deleting a receipt → MUST move its photo to the DMS trash (`ReceiptCaptureService::trashPhoto()`, after the commit) only when `delete()` returned a document id; MUST NOT delete a document another receipt (a split part) still names
- When a template or payment method is no longer wanted → MUST deactivate it with `setActive(…, false)`; MUST NOT delete the row or change its code (receipts carry it)
- When a receipt is tied to a bank movement or untied → MUST go through `ReceiptBankMatchService::take()` / `ReceiptJournalService::takeMovement()` and `releaseMovement()` (financial's `BankLinkService` inside the receipt's unit of work, the receipt row locked first); MUST NOT write `bank_link` from this module, link without the office's act (a receipt has no identity level) or link a receipt that was not paid through a bank account
- When offering bank movements for a receipt → MUST offer only FREE debit movements of the bank account the receipt was paid through (`bankAccountsOf()`), and tick one in advance only when it is the unique proposal; MUST NOT tick a foreign-currency candidate or pick among several
- When a linked receipt is changed → MUST refuse a change of amount or «Haben» and a split until the link is released, and MUST release the link in the same unit of work when the receipt is deleted (ADR-061 decision 10); MUST NOT leave a link to a receipt that no longer exists
- When the day window of the bank match is needed → MUST read `receiptConfig['bankMatch']` through `ReceiptBankMatchService`; MUST NOT write day counts into a template, a help text or another service
- When a value of a receipt or of master data is checked → MUST use `ReceiptFields` (date, amount, text, account, tax code); MUST NOT add a second rule set in a screen or the API target
- When a document (receipt, creditor invoice) names a foreign currency → MUST take the allowed currencies from `ReceiptFields::foreignCurrencies()` (`receiptConfig['foreignCurrencies']`, the ONE list) and check through `foreignCurrency()` / `originalAmount()`; MUST NOT add a second list or config key
- When a receipt screen shows the photo → MUST take its addresses from `ReceiptPhoto` (module-dms `MediaField::previewUrl()` / `thumbUrl()`, the read-gated preview); MUST NOT write a `/document/preview` address by hand (harness S7 / S8)
- When a screen shows VAT totals of receipts → MUST read them from `ReceiptJournalPostingService::preview()` (`vat`, `withoutVat`); MUST NOT compute a VAT share in a template or a controller
- When a receipt target answers → MUST refuse a caller without `$request->device()` (403) and take who captured from the device principal; MUST NOT put an account or a VAT code into a payload for the phone (E2, E10)
- When a tile symbol is added to `ReceiptTemplate::ICONS` → MUST add its glyph to `ReceiptIcons::GLYPHS` (24×24 stroke path) in the same change; MUST NOT reference an external icon file from the app screen (the app CSP and the inline sprite)
- When the phone screen shows server data → MUST build it with DOM nodes and `textContent` (`_Z77.app.shell.el()`); MUST NOT use `innerHTML` or inline script/style (the app CSP is enforced)
- When changing who reads the receipt photos besides the SUPER_USER → MUST change `receiptConfig['dmsPartitionGrants']` (since 2026-11-20: role `admin`, `read` on «Belege»); module-dms applies it once per installation ([`documents.md`](documents.md) DMS-MODULE-ACL-001); MUST NOT call `grantRole()` from this module.

## known issues

- **RECEIPT-VAT-RATE-001** — don't assume the input tax of a receipt uses the rate printed on it: `GrossEntryBuilder` resolves the rate at the ENTRY date. Wrong only around a change of rate (a voucher of the old rate captured after the change); left as it is by the owner on 2026-11-24, to be fixed before the next change — the same rule as CREDITOR-VAT-RATE-001 (`creditor.md`).
- RECEIPT-ORPHAN-001: don't assume a stored photo always has a receipt — the photo is written to the DMS (file store) BEFORE the receipt row is committed; a failing insert (e.g. two parallel first sends with the same `capture_key`) leaves the photo in `Belege/<year>/<month>` without a row. Harmless (the Drive shows it), not cleaned up automatically
- RECEIPT-PHONE-001: don't assume an iPhone photo arrives — the phone converts it to JPEG with `createImageBitmap` + canvas; where a browser cannot decode the file, the screen says «Dieses Bild kann das Handy nicht lesen» and nothing is sent (HEIC is refused by the server anyway, T2). Not tested on a real iPhone yet (api.md API-008)
- RECEIPT-KEY-001: don't assume a repeated `capture_key` is compared — the existing receipt is returned even when the repeat carries other values (the same key means the same capture, the gateway's idempotency model)
- RECEIPT-BUILD-001 (resolved 2026-11-15, creditor plan §2.5): don't assume the receipt's lines are this module's own — they come from financial's public `GrossEntryBuilder`, shared with the one-line entry and module-creditor; a change of its split changes every receipt posting. `tests/financial-gross-entry.php` pins the lines (and refusal messages) the receipt produced BEFORE the extraction
- RECEIPT-VAT-001 — resolved 2026-11-15 (owner decision, as the creditor journal): `ReceiptPostingBuilder::lines()` hands the mandator's `isLiableToVat()` to `GrossEntryBuilder::build()` — a mandator NOT liable to VAT refuses every tax code at posting («ohne MWST» required); no mandator yet → not judged there. Verified: `tests/module-receipt.php` RV1, the strict mode itself in `tests/financial-gross-entry.php`.
- RECEIPT-PM-COUNT-001 — resolved 2026-11-15 (creditor 1c): the payment methods' usage count (column «Verwendet», the line in the window) is `Services\PaymentMethodUsage` — the receipts plus every module's `paymentMethodUsage` registration (`PaymentMethodUsageInterface`; module-creditor registers its invoices). Receipt names no module. There is still no delete of a payment method, only «Aktiv». Verified: `tests/module-creditor.php` C14.
- RECEIPT-PAIR-001: don't assume a template and a payment method are judged together before a receipt exists — a template whose debit and a payment method whose credit are BOTH P&L accounts, with a VAT code, cannot post (the one-line entry refuses it); the preview shows it as blocked per receipt, master-data saving does not refuse it
- RECEIPT-DMS-001: don't assume the DMS rule «no `saveGenerated()` for a person's upload» ([`documents.md`](documents.md), media picker) is broken by accident — the phone photo is a person's upload on the trusted path by approved decision T2; the capture service carries the checks (sniff, allowlist, size) that `UploadService` would
- RECEIPT-RETAIN-001: don't assume a posted receipt's photo has its `retentionUntil` the moment the posting commits — the DMS write runs after the commit; a failure is logged (`receipt retention after posting: …`) and healed by the next «Journal verbuchen» or by opening «Verbuchte Journale». A photo moved OUT of the «Belege» partition in the Drive (or a `moduleFolders` remap since) is not written by the module handle (ADR-021 rule 4) — `apply()` returns its id in `skipped`, nobody is told
- RECEIPT-BANK-001: don't assume a receipt links by itself — there is no reference on a slip, so every link is the office's act in «Zahlungen suchen …» / «Zahlung suchen …»; an unlinked card movement stays «zu prüfen» in «Bankkonto» and — on a bank-driven account, i.e. «Kontoauszug einlesen» on and from «Abgleich ab» (stage C4, [`bank.md`](bank.md)) — blocks the year close; before that date, or without it, the movement is information only and has no state (it can still be linked)
- RECEIPT-BANK-002: don't assume the original foreign amount is kept on the receipt — receipt FX (F1–F14) is not built; a foreign candidate taken overwrites the amount with the bank's CHF, the original stands only on the linked movement (shown as «Bank · date · CHF · 50.00 EUR») and on the photo. After «Zuordnung lösen» the amount stays the CHF
- RECEIPT-BANK-003: don't assume the receipt date follows the bank — a linked receipt still posts on its RECEIPT date, the movement is booked days later; across a period end the ledger change and the business movements of stage C4's check fall into different periods and BOTH periods fail and block ([`bank.md`](bank.md) BANK-CHECK-002; owner question, `## pending`). Inside one period the pair is joined: financial's check asks `Services/ReceiptBankEntryOwner` (`receiptConfig → bankEntryOwners`) — an entry of origin `receipt` / receipt id belongs to the link `receipt:{id}`
- RECEIPT-BANK-004: don't assume a receipt on a bank-driven account is refused or held without a link — unlike creditor's «Bezahlt am» nothing is locked: the receipt posts as always, and financial's check then names it «Buchung ohne Bewegung» until «Zahlungen suchen …» links it (the alarm is the check, not the Belegjournal)
- RECEIPT-BANK-004: don't assume a split receipt can be linked as a whole — the parts are separate receipts with part amounts, the movement carries the sum; one link per movement over its full amount (bank.md BANK-LINK-001) fits none of them. Link first is no way out either (a linked receipt refuses the split)
- RECEIPT-BANK-005: don't assume the merchant is compared cleverly — a word of the receipt's text (≥ 4 characters, letters and digits, lower case) must stand as a whole word in the bank text; «Bäckerei» does not find «BAECKEREI». It only ranks and breaks ties, the amount and the date decide
- RECEIPT-BANK-006: don't assume the link of a posted receipt can be taken back — `releaseMovement()` refuses it until the correction of a posted receipt exists (below)
