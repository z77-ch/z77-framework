## mental model

`z77/module-financial` (plan §5, ADR-040, ADR-042) is the double-entry bookkeeping every posting source reaches through one door, `LedgerService::post()` — it knows no module. Built in three parts: **part 1** the chart of accounts and the fiscal years with their periods; **part 2** the journal (`JournalEntry` / `JournalLine`), `LedgerService` (`post()`, `reverse()`), manual entries with their change log (`EntryChange`, `ManualEntryService`) and the journal screens; **part 3 (this state)** the reports — trial balance, balance sheet, income statement, account statement, journal — as SQL aggregates (`LedgerReports`, `JournalLineRepository`) with one backend page each. The VAT return, the period transitions and the year-end are P5. All entities are Doctrine (`#[Entity('doctrine')]`, announced in `financialConfig.inc.php` → `doctrineEntities`); the package depends on kernel, `persistence-doctrine` and `module-vat` (tax codes are validated against it and the manual form resolves rates through `VatRates`) and requires PHP 8.4.

### Part 1 — chart of accounts, fiscal years

- **`Account`** (table `account`: `number` unique, `name`, `type`, `parent_id` FK to `account`, `postable`, `active`). The number is digits only, at most 10, stored as a string — string order is the chart's order (`1` < `10` < `100` < `1000` < `1020` < `106` < … < `11`), which `AccountRepository::allInOrder()` uses and the list indents by. Grouping is a plain parent reference, no nested set. A **group** (KMU class `1`, main group `10`, group `100`) is an account with `postable = false`; only postable AND active accounts are accepted on a journal line (`PostingRules`).
- **Five types** (`AccountType`: `asset`, `liability`, `equity`, `expense`, `revenue` — owner decision 2026-09-22): equity is its own type so the balance sheet splits equity from debt by type, without a number rule. German labels live in the UI (`ACCOUNT_TYPE_LABELS` in the trait: Aktiven, Fremdkapital, Eigenkapital, Aufwand, Ertrag). A group carries a type as well; for a group mixing types (KMU classes 7 and 8, group 69 with `6950 Finanzertrag`) it is informational.
- **Validation** (`AccountValidator`): number required, digits, unique; name required; type one of the five; the parent must be a GROUP, never the account itself or one of its descendants (the validator walks the chain upwards, with a visited set against a cycle already in the data); a group WITH children cannot become postable; the journal locks on `type` and `postable` (below, FIN-TYPE-001). Deliberately no rule tying the number to the parent's number or the type to the parent's type — the KMU chart mixes types inside a class, and its groups are number RANGES, not prefixes (`1020` sits in group `100`, `1850` in `180`).
- **Write side = `AccountService`**: `save($new)`, `update($account, $values)` (the parent travels as the entity under the key `parent`), `setActive()`, `adoptKmuChart()`. `update()` validates a detached `clone` first and applies the values to the managed entity only when it passed (ADR-039 decision 9, the `ContactService` model); a refused change never reaches the next flush. The **number is immutable** (`AccountNumberChangedException`): settings and other modules' configuration name accounts by number (plan §5.1, §5.4), and so does every `PostingLine`. No delete anywhere — an account is deactivated; journal lines reference it by FK. A unique-number race in `save()` becomes the validator's field error (`uniq_account_number`).
- **Journal locks on `type` and `postable`** (owner decision 2026-09-22, FIN-TYPE-001). Why: the reports read the account TYPE at runtime (`LedgerReports::section()`), so a type change moves every past posting of the account between balance sheet and income statement — closed years included; after the close nothing changes. Hence: (1) `type` may change as long as NO journal line of the account lies in a `closed` period — «closed year» = periods in state `closed` (the P5 year-end closes the periods; there is no year state); `vat-settled` does not lock. Afterwards refused, and the German message names the correction: a new account with the wanted type plus a manual transfer entry (Umbuchung). (2) `postable` true → false (the account becomes a group) is refused as soon as ANY journal line references it, in any period state — a group never carries lines. (3) `name` and `active` stay free; the number was immutable already. The rules live in `AccountValidator` (`validateType()` / `validatePostable()`, fed by the service with the STORED type and postable and `JournalLineRepository`; messages `AccountValidator::TYPE_LOCKED` / `POSTABLE_LOCKED`). The queries (Doctrine-only SQL): `accountHasLines()` — one probe of the FK index on `account_id`, `LIMIT 1`; `accountHasLinesInClosedPeriod()` — first the closed periods (a few dozen rows), merged into one date range per run of adjacent closed periods of a year (none closed → no journal query at all), then per range the account-statement shape (`account_id`, `fiscal_year_id`, `entry_date BETWEEN`) with `LIMIT 1`. The ranges are probed NEWEST first — the latest closed year is where an account in use has lines, so the common case stops at the first range. Measured: ≈ 1 ms in the harness (section P, 20'000 lines: hit; miss; an account with 3'333 lines none of which lies in the — small — closed year) and ad hoc at 200'000 lines (the harness is not parametrised). The review measured the expensive case: a busy account against a closed 100'000-entry year that holds none of its lines costs ≈ 0.5 s per such year (the optimizer walks the entry side), linear in the number of closed years it has to pass — with oldest-first probing every closed year before the one with a hit. Newest-first makes the common case one range; a MISS (a type change on an account with lines only in open periods, many closed years) still pays every closed year. A single join over all closed periods at once was worse (it walked every line of a busy account, 0.7 s at 200'000 ad hoc). No composite index; the structural fix is the `type_locked` marker of P5 (pending).
- **How `AccountService::update()` checks them — validate before mutate, twice.** A change of `type` or `postable` true → false is validated first LOCK-FREE on the detached draft against the managed entity's state — the common refusal, nothing touched, the managed entity unmodified and no later flush writes it. When that passes, the change runs in a unit of work of its own (`getTransaction(Account::class)->run()`): FIRST statement `AccountRepository::lockForUpdate()` (`SELECT type, postable … FOR UPDATE`), then the validator AGAIN against that row, then the managed entity is mutated and the commit flushes it. The guarded path refuses to run inside an open unit of work (`LogicException`) — its re-check must read after the lock, not from a caller's older snapshot (InnoDB's REPEATABLE READ snapshot starts at the first plain read). Name, active and group take the plain flush path as before. A refusal UNDER the lock (only in a race) rolls the unit of work back, which replaces the EntityManager (DOCTRINE-TX-004): the account handed in is detached and serves the form only — the screen re-reads by id.
- **The race with a posting, and the lock order.** The FK insert of `journal_line` share-locks the account only at the caller's FLUSH, after `post()` validated the account on a plain read — an account turned into a group in between would get the line. So `post()` re-checks its accounts on a LOCKING read right AFTER drawing the number (`PostingRules::lockAccounts()` → `AccountRepository::lockForPosting()`, `SELECT … LOCK IN SHARE MODE`, the latest committed postable / active, held until commit); the manual edit does the same after `resolveAccounts()` (it draws no number). Lock order: `NumberRange` → accounts (shared) in a posting, the account alone (exclusive) in `update()`, which takes nothing after it — no cycle (ADR-039 decision 10 unchanged: `NumberRange` first). Two outcomes, both proven by forced two-process probes in the harness (T14, T16), each failing without the shared lock: a posting in flight holds the row shared → `update()`'s lock waits for its commit and the re-check refuses on its line; `update()` holds the row → the posting (lock-free check passed, number drawn) waits, reads the account as a group and is refused with `account-not-postable` — its rollback gives the number back. The type rule has no such race today: nothing posts into a `closed` period, and no period transition exists before P5 (see pending — the `type_locked` marker closes it).
- **Side effect, accepted (review 2026-09-22):** a posting holds its accounts' rows SHARED until its commit, so ANY change of such an account — name, active, parent included, whose flush updates the row — waits behind an in-flight posting on the same account. Normally milliseconds; a long unit of work (an import posting thousands of entries in one `run()`) can delay a rename up to `innodb_lock_wait_timeout` (50 s by default), after which the rename fails with the driver's exception.
- **The screen**: the edit form asks `AccountService::postingLocks()` (lock-free) and shows the type select DISABLED with the reason when the type is locked, and «Bebuchbar» disabled without the option «Nein — Gruppe» when a postable account carries lines. A disabled select is not submitted, so the stored value stays; after a refusal a locked select shows the STORED type (`storedType`), not the refused draft's. The server refusal is the real guard. No JavaScript.
- **KMU chart by button, not by seed** (owner decision 2026-09-22): `res/charts/kmu.json` (UTF-8 without BOM, a list of `{number, name, type, postable, parent}` with parents before children) is the Swiss SME chart in the Sterchi structure — classes 1–9, main groups, groups and 196 rows with the common postable accounts, German names. `adoptKmuChart()` refuses unless the chart is EMPTY (`AccountRepository::isEmpty()`, any row counts, active or not — `ChartNotEmptyException`), validates every row before persisting any, and writes all in ONE flush; two adoptions racing collide on the unique number and the loser writes nothing. Rationale: an installation that migrates its wdv chart (P5b) must not get a second chart forced into it. Owner decisions 2026-09-22 on the content: class 28 ships in the **legal-entity variant only** («Eigenkapital (juristische Personen)») — a sole proprietorship adds Kapital / Privat by hand in the backend, there is no second chart file; class 9 «Abschluss» carries its main groups only — its postable closing accounts arrive with the year-end in P5.
- **`FiscalYear`** (table `fiscal_year`: `code` unique, `start_date` unique, `end_date`) and **`Period`** (table `fiscal_period`: `fiscal_year_id` FK, `start_date`, `end_date`, `state`; unique on year + start). Owner decisions 2026-09-22: FREE start and end dates (deviating, shortened, extended years), at most 24 months; years are contiguous — a new year starts the day after the latest one ends, the first may start anywhere; periods are CALENDAR MONTHS clipped to the year (15.3.–31.12. → ten periods, the first 15.3.–31.3.). `FiscalYear::covers($date)` and `periodOn($date)` (a walk over the dozen periods, no query) and `FiscalYearRepository::findByDate($date)` (DQL) are how the ledger finds the year and period of a posting date.
- **The code** names the year and its number range `journal-entry.{code}` (`FiscalYear::journalEntryRange()`, the one place it is composed): lower-case kebab (`2026`, `2026-27`), at most 16 characters, unique, proposed from the dates (`FiscalYearService::proposeCode()`: `2026` inside one calendar year, `2026-27` across). A code is needed rather than the start year because two years can start in the same calendar year (short year 1.1.–30.6.2026, then 1.7.2026–30.6.2027) — orchestrator's follow-on decision to the owner's free dates. Immutable: `FiscalYear` has no public setter (code and dates come with the constructor), and there is no edit of a year — a wrongly opened one is deleted while still empty (below) and opened anew. An empty code is reported only when both dates are valid — otherwise the code would have been proposed from them, and the date errors say what to fix.
- **Opening a year** (`FiscalYearService::open()`): validate (`FiscalYearValidator`), derive the monthly periods, then ONE unit of work through `UnifiedEntityManager::getTransaction(FiscalYear::class)->run()` — first `getRepository(NumberRange::class)->create('journal-entry.{code}')` (lock order: `NumberRange` first), then `persist()` of the year with its periods (cascade). The range is created at 0 and committed with the year, so the first draw under contention never takes the new-row path (DOCTRINE-NR-003); an exception anywhere rolls back year, periods and range together. The range must be NEW: `create()` answers `false` for a row that already exists (a leftover of a removed year, an import), and continuing it would start the year's entries at its old number + 1 unnoticed — refused as a `code` field error, rolled back (review M1). `open()` OWNS its unit of work and refuses to run inside an open one (`LogicException`): nested, the flush would happen at the caller's commit, where the race mapping can no longer turn a unique-index violation into a field error (review L1) — the production caller is the backend controller. After the range, `open()` locks every fiscal-year row (`FiscalYearRepository::lockAll()`, `SELECT … FOR UPDATE`, a handful of rows) and runs the validator AGAIN under that lock — `delete()` takes the same lock, so a predecessor deleted between the first validation and the commit is seen and the opening is refused on `start_date` instead of leaving a gap (FIN-FY-002). Two openings of the same next year on a NON-empty table are serialised by that lock: the second one ALWAYS fails the re-validation and gets a `start_date` field error (a row written past the validator that shares the start date still collides on `uniq_fiscal_year_start`, the same field error — D28). On an EMPTY table the two openings of the first year deadlock instead (FIN-FY-003); the screen answers the loser with a sentence.
- **Deleting a wrongly opened year** (owner decision 2026-09-22, FIN-FY-002 — `FiscalYearService::delete(int $yearId)`): allowed only for the **latest** year (a middle one would break contiguity) and only while **nothing was ever posted** in it — no `journal_entry` references it, no `journal_entry_change` row of it exists (a deleted manual entry leaves one AND a consumed number; its gap history must not be orphaned), and its range `journal-entry.{code}` still stands at `last_number = 0`. Removes the year, its periods and the range in ONE unit of work through `getTransaction()->run()`, and refuses to run inside an open one (`LogicException`, the `open()` model). The existence of a change row is asked with `findOneBy()` (one row, no hydration of the whole log). Everything is decided INSIDE the unit of work, under locks, so a posting cannot slip in between: first the range row — `NumberRangeRepository::dropUnused()` locks it (`FOR UPDATE`) and deletes it only at 0 (lock order: `NumberRange` FIRST, DOCTRINE-NR-002; a poster of the year either committed before, and the range is no longer at 0, or waits; after the delete's commit its `next()` re-creates the range at 0 and draws 1, its `journal_entry` insert then fails on the foreign key to the deleted year, and its rollback removes the re-created range row again — nothing is written. The journal's «Buchung erfassen» answers that failure with «Das Geschäftsjahr wurde inzwischen gelöscht» — recognised by the key's name `JournalEntry::FK_FISCAL_YEAR` down the exception chain (`Ui/RaceFailure`), no other foreign-key violation (Y18–Y20)); then every fiscal-year row (`lockAll()`, against an opening of the next year racing the delete); then the checks on the fresh state. A refusal (`FiscalYearNotDeletableException`, reasons `not-found`, `not-latest`, `has-entries`, `had-entries`, `range-used`) or any failure rolls back the whole unit of work — the range row is back as it was. The code is free again afterwards: the year re-opens with the same code and a NEW range at 0. `deletionRefusal($year)` is the lock-free read the screen asks (it does not read the range — through the ledger a committed number always leaves an entry or a change row; `delete()` checks the range anyway). The screen: «Löschen …» on the latest year's section header, shown only while `deletionRefusal()` is null; `confirm-delete?id=` (modal, `data-fetch-get`, says why when refused) and `delete` (`#[Fetch, HttpMethod('POST')]`, entity token `fiscalYear`, success redirects to the list). Verified: Y1–Y16 (latest empty year deleted and re-opened with the same code, not-latest, an entry, a created and deleted manual entry, a drawn number, a rollback at the flush, nested).
- **Period state** (`PeriodState`: `open`, `vat-settled`, `closed` — ADR-042 decision 10): every period is created `open`; there is no transition and no state setter yet (P5). The ledger refuses by it (part 2, below); the harness sets states directly in SQL to prove the refusals.

### Part 2 — journal, LedgerService, manual entries

- **`JournalEntry`** (table `journal_entry`, ADR-042 decision 5): `fiscal_year_id` FK, `number` (unique per year — `uniq_journal_entry_number`), `entry_date` (not `date`, a MariaDB function name), `text`, `kind` (`EntryKind`: `generated` | `manual`), `source_type` + `source_ref` (the OPAQUE origin, ADR-040 decision 2 — null on a manual entry), `idempotency_key` (unique `uniq_journal_entry_idempotency`, null on a manual entry — MariaDB allows many NULLs), `reversal_of_id` (self FK, unique `uniq_journal_entry_reversal_of` — **an entry is reversed at most once, in the schema**; a `ManyToOne` plus a named unique index rather than `OneToOne`, so the index carries our name and `diff` stays clean), `created_by` / `created_at`, `changed_by` / `changed_at`, and `version` (`#[ORM\Version]`, the optimistic lock of a manual edit — below). Index `(fiscal_year_id, entry_date)` for the reports. The period is NOT stored — it is derived from the date (`FiscalYear::periodOn()`), and period bounds never move. No setter; the one mutation is `amend()` for a manual edit, and it refuses a generated entry itself. `snapshot()` (the change log's before/after) carries the header incl. `created_by/at` and `changed_by/at`, and the lines; the idempotency comparison picks its own keys out of it and ignores the audit fields.
- **`JournalLine`** (table `journal_line`): `entry_id` FK (cascade persist + remove, `orphanRemoval`), `position` (from 1, deliberately NOT unique — Doctrine inserts before it deletes, and an edit that replaces the lines would collide with itself), `account_id` FK, `debit` / `credit` (`MoneyType`, `DECIMAL(15,2)`, exactly one positive, the other `0.00` — the entity constructor guards it too), tax data `tax_code` (VARCHAR 8, indexed) / `tax_rate` (INT, hundredths of a percent) / `tax_base` / `tax_amount` (money, nullable) — **all-or-none**, on the NET line only (revenue or expense), never on the tax-account line or the counter line: the VAT return (P5) is Σ `tax_base` and Σ `tax_amount` per code and rate, so exactly one line per taxed amount carries them. `text` nullable. No currency (ADR-042 decision 4).
- **Tax fields — what ADR-041 says and what is stored.** ADR-041 decision 7 names `taxCode`, `taxBase`, `taxAmount`; the line stores those AND a **`taxRate` snapshot** (plan §5.6: the return groups by code «+ rate», and two rates of one code meet in one period around a rate change — 2023/2024; `vat.md`'s rule for a Doctrine reference to a tax code asks for the rate snapshot as well) — recorded as a dated addendum to ADR-041, approved by the owner 2026-09-22. The label is NOT snapshotted (label snapshots belong to documents; a ledger line reports by code + rate, not by label). The tax code is referenced BY CODE with no foreign key (ADR-043 decision 19). **Base and amount are SIGNED** as the poster states them: a credit note or a reversal carries them negated, so the return can sum the column without asking which side the account was on. Verified: a sale and its reversal sum to `0.00` (`tests/module-financial.php` H8).
- **`EntryChange`** (table `journal_entry_change`, ADR-042 decisions 7 and 9, Q4): `entry_id`, `fiscal_year_id`, `entry_number` as PLAIN columns — **no foreign key**, so the row of a DELETED entry survives and documents the gap in the numbering; `action` (`ChangeAction`: `update` | `delete`), `changed_by` / `changed_at`, `before_snapshot` / `after_snapshot` (`JournalEntry::snapshot()` as JSON in TEXT columns — TEXT rather than the platform JSON type so the schema diff does not depend on how a MariaDB version reports a JSON check; `after` null for a delete). Immutable: constructor and getters. Creation is not logged — the entry carries created by/at.
- **`Ledger/` — the DTOs other modules see** (plan §5.4): `PostingRequest` (`generated(date, text, sourceType, sourceRef, idempotencyKey, lines)` / `manual(date, text, lines)`, private constructor), `PostingLine` (`debit(account, amount, …)` / `credit(…)`, the account by NUMBER, optional `taxCode` + `taxRate` + `taxBase` + `taxAmount` + `text`), `EntryRef` (`fiscalYear` code + `number` — no database id; how a module STORES it is its own shape, P3). Immutable and validated on construction (`InvalidArgumentException` = a programming error of the caller): at least two lines, one currency, Σ debit = Σ credit, one positive side per line, tax data all-or-none, a generated request names its origin and carries a key, a manual one carries no key.
- **`LedgerService::post(PostingRequest): EntryRef`** — the one write path (plan §1, ADR-040 decisions 2 and 8, ADR-042 decision 6). Asserts `getTransaction(JournalEntry::class)->isOpen()` and never opens a unit of work (`LogicException` otherwise). Order inside: (1) **idempotency**, a read — a generated request whose key exists returns the existing `EntryRef` when the CONTENT is the same, and throws `IdempotencyConflictException` (a `PostingRefusedException`, reason `idempotency-conflict`, naming the existing entry) when it differs. «Content» = `PostingRequest::fingerprint()`: date, text, origin and the lines in order (account, debit, credit, tax code/rate/base/amount, text) — everything the module decides; compared with `fingerprintOf($entry->snapshot())`. Why loud: returning the old entry silently would hide that the source and the books disagree, posting again would post twice — both are worse than an exception at the caller. (2) **the rules** (`PostingRules`, `@internal`, shared with the manual edit): the fiscal year and period for the date (`no-fiscal-year`, `no-period`), the period state (`closed` → `period-closed`, `vat-settled` with a tax line → `period-vat-settled`), every account exists AND is postable AND active (`account-unknown`, `account-not-postable`, `account-inactive`), every tax code EXISTS in module-vat (`tax-code-unknown`), then the actor is resolved (an unnamed author refuses here). (3) **the number**, the FIRST write: `NumberRangeRepository::next($year->journalEntryRange())` — never before validation, so a refused posting never takes the range lock; right after it the accounts are re-checked under a shared row lock (`PostingRules::lockAccounts()`, FIN-TYPE-001 — refuses only in a race with «becomes a group» / a deactivation, and the refusal must propagate so the number goes back). (4) the entry with its lines, `persist()`ed; the caller's flush at commit writes it, a rolled-back caller consumes no number (verified: G18/G19, and gapless under three parallel processes with rollbacks, K).
- **Tax codes: exist vs. active.** `PostingRules::assertTaxCodesExist()` asks for EXISTENCE only. A posting carries the code its document snapshotted, and that document may be finalised or reversed after the code was deactivated (ADR-043 decision 19: deactivated, never deleted; history resolves). Offering only ACTIVE codes for a NEW document is the screen's job — the manual form does (`ManualEntryForm::selectableCodes()`: active codes plus, on an edit, the codes the entry already carries; a deactivated code may be kept, never newly chosen — the `contact.md` type rule).
- **Accounts: postable vs. active** (ADR-043 decision 19 applied to accounts, review 2026-09-22 M5 — «inactive» means not offered for NEW postings, existing references keep working): `post()` needs every account POSTABLE and ACTIVE; `reverse()` needs POSTABLE only — a counterpart of an existing entry must always be possible, also on an account deactivated since (`PostingRules::resolveAccounts($lines, requireActive: false)`); a manual edit needs an active account for every NEW or CHANGED line and lets an UNCHANGED line keep its now-inactive account (`requireActive` as a `list<bool>` by line — a line is unchanged when its fingerprint equals a stored line's: account, amounts, tax data, text). `ManualEntryForm::keepFrom($entry)` applies the same rule first, so the row shows the error («… ist inaktiv — nur eine unveränderte Zeile behält es»).
- **What a caller of `post()` / `reverse()` sees under a race** (review M4; the behaviour is kept, this is the contract): the idempotency lookup is a READ, so the same key posted in two parallel units of work — or twice inside ONE unit of work before its flush — passes the lookup twice and fails at the caller's COMMIT with Doctrine's `UniqueConstraintViolationException` on `uniq_journal_entry_idempotency`; two parallel reversals of one entry fail the same way (their key is `reversal:{year}/{number}`), and on `uniq_journal_entry_reversal_of` should the keys ever differ. The whole unit of work rolls back, **no number is consumed**, nothing is half-written. The caller lets it propagate and does not retry inside the unit of work (ADR-039 decision 10 — no retry loop); a retry is a NEW unit of work, and with the same key it then returns the existing entry.
- **A reversal may be dated in a LATER fiscal year** than the entry it reverses (review P12): a correction happens in an OPEN period (ADR-042 decision 10), the reversal takes the next number of the year it is dated in, and `reversalOf` links across the years (verified K5). The journal shows «Storno von {year}/{number}» with the reversed entry's own year.
- **`LedgerService::reverse(EntryRef, DateTimeImmutable $date, string $reason): EntryRef`** (plan §1 correction principle, ADR-042 decisions 7 and 8): a GENERATED counterpart with debit and credit swapped and tax base / amount NEGATED (`PostingLine::swapped()`), dated `$date`, **its text IS the reason** (owner-approved 2026-09-22: the reason is the reversal entry's text, no separate column — the journal shows it where every entry shows what happened), origin INHERITED from the reversed entry (the same source is being corrected; `reverse()` has no origin parameter, plan §5.4), key `reversal:{year}/{number}`, `reversalOf` set. The reversal's date goes through the same period rules as any posting. Refused (`ReversalRefusedException`, reasons): `not-found`, `manual` (a manual entry is edited or deleted, never reversed), `is-reversal` (a reversal is not reversed — post the original anew), `already-reversed` (the service checks, the unique index enforces under a race), `no-reason`. Same open-unit-of-work rule as `post()`.
- **Who** (`Services/Actor`): the framework knows the current identity as `AuthUser` (`AuthService::getCurrentUser()`) — a logged-in backend user (realm `backend`, the username) or, in the CLI, the job runner's actor (realm `cron`, `cron:{jobKey}`, ADR-031). Both are NAMES, and the books keep the name (`created_by`, `changed_by`, the change log; VARCHAR 80) — not an id (a backend user can be deleted, the entry stays). `Actor::current()` REFUSES to guess (`LogicException`) when nobody is logged in or no `AuthService` is wired: a CLI script, the import (P5b) and the harness pass the actor explicitly (`new LedgerService($em, 'import:wdv')`). A posting without a named author is what the change log exists to prevent.
- **`ManualEntryService`** (owner, 2026-09-22): `create(PostingRequest $manual): EntryRef` (a manual request through `LedgerService::post()` in a unit of work opened HERE), `update(int $entryId, int $expectedVersion, PostingRequest $new)`, `delete(int $entryId, int $expectedVersion)`. Each method OWNS its unit of work and refuses to run inside an open one (`LogicException`, the `FiscalYearService::open()` model) — a caller that wants many manual entries in one transaction (the import) uses `LedgerService::post()` with `PostingRequest::manual()` directly. Rules for edit and delete (`EntryNotEditableException`, reasons): a GENERATED entry → `generated` (in the domain — `JournalEntry::amend()` refuses as well); period `open` → allowed; `vat-settled` → allowed only if NEITHER the old NOR the new version carries a tax line (`period-vat-settled`; the filed return summed the tax lines); `closed` → `period-closed`; an edit whose new date lies in ANOTHER fiscal year → `fiscal-year-changed` (the number belongs to the year — delete and re-create). Both the OLD period (where the entry is) and the NEW period (where the date moves) are checked. The new version arrives as a `PostingRequest` — structurally valid by construction. Inside `run()`: the entry is RE-READ with its header row locked (`JournalEntryRepository::lockForUpdate()`, `SELECT … FOR UPDATE`) and its version compared with `$expectedVersion` — gone or another version → `EntryConflictException`; then every database rule (`PostingRules`: year, period, accounts, tax codes) runs on the FRESH entity; only then is it touched (ADR-039 decision 9, DOCTRINE-TX-007): `EntryChange(update, before, after)` + `amend()` (date, text, lines replaced — the old lines leave the collection, orphan removal deletes them at flush — `changed_by` / `changed_at` stamped), or `EntryChange(delete, before, null)` + `remove()`. A delete leaves a GAP: the number is not reused (verified I22–I25).
- **Concurrency of manual edits** (review 2026-09-22 H1/M1–M3, decided: optimistic locking): `journal_entry.version` (`#[ORM\Version]`) travels as a hidden field of the edit form and the delete modal; the service takes id + expected version, never a pre-loaded entity. Two guards, one exception: the version check on the locked re-read catches a change that happened BEFORE the write (a second editor waits on the row lock, then reads the committed state — version bumped or row gone), and Doctrine's `OptimisticLockException` at flush (mapped to the same `EntryConflictException`) catches the rest. A manual edit ALWAYS bumps the version, also when only the lines change, because `amend()` stamps `changed_at` and the header row is updated (verified I13b). The screen answers a conflict with «Die Buchung wurde inzwischen geändert oder gelöscht — bitte neu laden» and a redirect — never a 500. Verified: two parallel processes editing one entry → one `ok`, one `conflict`, two lines not four, version 2, one change row (I27/I28); a stale delete after a delete and a stale edit after a delete → `EntryConflictException`, one change row (I23b/I23c).
- **The screens**: the fragment pattern (ADR-018) like the accounts and the fiscal years — `Ui/JournalControllerTrait` + `JournalLayout::config()`, templates in `res/view/templates/Backend/JournalController/`, the host `module-backend` `Finance/JournalController`, hc1 («Buchung erfassen», a LINK to the entry page, `addButton`) and hc2 (the fiscal-year switcher, one link per year — the `.be-lang-switch` anatomy, `yearSwitch`) as partials OF THE FRAGMENT, added by `listAction()` (see «fragment slots» below). URLs `/backend/finance/journal/list?year={code}` (the year containing today by default, else the latest; bounded by `journalListLimit`, «n von m angezeigt»; a second section lists the deleted numbers with who and when, from the change log), `detail?id=` (header, lines with tax data, reversal link in both directions — «Storno von» names the reversed entry's OWN year —, the change log with before/after as disclosure rows — checkbox, no JavaScript), `add?year=` and `edit?id=` (PAGES with a plain `<form method="post">`, `#[Csrf]` + `csrf_token`; an edit also checks the entity token `journalEntry` and carries `version`; the edit page of a NON-editable entry — generated, or period closed — shows the same refusal the detail view shows and goes back there), `confirm-delete?id=` (modal, `data-fetch-get`, carries `version`) and `delete` (`#[Fetch, HttpMethod('POST')]`, entity token; success redirects to the journal through the envelope). The pages other than the list swap the pinned body section (`removeSection('main')` + `addPartials()`) — the layout config pins `listAction`. NO reverse button: the module that posted an entry reverses it (ADR-042 decision 7). Generated entries are shown only. The base currency the form parses amounts in is read in ONE helper (`LedgerService::baseCurrency()`, `systemConfig → baseCurrency`, the same key the Doctrine bootstrap hands `MoneyType`; `JournalControllerTrait::journalCurrency()` delegates, and so do the reports since part 3) — `MoneyType` exposes no getter, and asking it would tie the form to the driver having booted first.
- **The manual-entry form** (`Ui/ManualEntryForm`, no JavaScript — Rule 7): date, text, n rows (account NUMBER typed with a shared `<datalist>` of the postable active accounts, text, debit OR credit — `100`, `100.50`, `100,50`, `1'000.00` accepted, `Money::fromDecimal()` — and an optional tax code from a `<select>`). Four rows to start; «Weitere Zeilen» is a second submit button (`op=more`) the server answers with three more rows; the balance (Σ Soll, Σ Haben, Differenz) and the computed VAT per line are shown after EVERY submit. VAT (ADR-041 decision 7, net method): the bookkeeper puts the code on the NET line only; the form resolves the rate for the ENTRY DATE (`VatRates::resolve()` — a manual entry's date is its service date), base = the line's amount, tax = base × rate rounded once (`Money::multiplyByRatio()`), and shows «MWST VM 8.1 %: 8.10 auf 100.00» so the bookkeeper enters the tax-account line (1170 / 2200) by hand like any other line; the balance check catches a wrong one. **Sign convention** (lives in the form, not in the ledger): base and amount positive when the line stands on the code's NATURAL side — credit for an output code, debit for an input code (`TaxCategory` `input-material` / `input-other`) — negative on the opposite side, so a manual revenue reduction posts a negative base and the return sums correctly. Amounts are shown through `Ui/AmountFormat` (Swiss apostrophe grouping, string work on `Money::toDecimal()`, no float).
- **`journalListLimit`** (financialConfig, optional, positive int, default 200 — `LedgerService::listLimit()`, the `ContactService::listLimit()` model): not set in the package config on purpose (Rule 2); a project sets it in its override copy (BOOT-CONFIG-001 applies).
- **Migration** `Version20260922091711` (`res/migrations`): generated with `z77-db diff` against a database at the part-1 migration, reviewed — `utf8mb4_unicode_ci`, `ENGINE = InnoDB` spelled out, the Doctrine-named indexes and FKs kept so `diff` stays clean, expand only, no DROP. The `version` column of the review fix was folded into it BEFORE it was ever committed (the module-contact precedent), so part 2 ships one migration. `tests/module-financial.php` A applies both migrations through the `z77-db` application and proves `diff` reports «No changes» afterwards (money, version and TEXT snapshot columns included).
- **Where the package is required**: the monorepo ROOT `composer.json` only (so `vendor/` carries it for the harness); NOT `skeleton/composer.json` — a project requires it when it needs it, like `module-contact`. No `RUNTIME=` paths therefore. The module has no routes and no view area (`financialConfig.inc.php` carries no `defaultGroup`).
- **The navigation entries are NOT in the kernel seed** (a host entry that fatals without the module would be wrong on a fresh install): a project adds «Kontenplan» → `/backend/finance/account/list`, «Geschäftsjahre» → `/backend/finance/fiscal-year/list`, «Journal» → `/backend/finance/journal/list` and «Auswertungen» → `/backend/finance/report` in the backend, like the tax codes and contacts.

### Part 3 — reports

- **The path**: `Ui/ReportControllerTrait` (host `Finance/ReportController`) → `Services/LedgerReports` (read-only, no unit of work) → SQL on `connection()` in `JournalLineRepository` (aggregates) and `JournalEntryRepository` (the journal report's page) — every method marked Doctrine-only (ADR-039 decision 8), every value BOUND (LIMIT/OFFSET too, `ParameterType::INTEGER`). Result types in `Reports/` (`TrialBalance`, `BalanceSheet`, `IncomeStatement`, `AccountStatement`, `JournalReport`, their rows, `ReportRange`, `Paging`) — immutable, no entity writes.
- **Never hydrated lines** (plan §5.5, §12/§13): one aggregate, `JournalLineRepository::balancesByAccount(year, from, to)` — Σ debit / Σ credit per account over `journal_entry` ⋈ `journal_line` ⋈ `account`, only accounts with lines, in number order — feeds the trial balance, the balance sheet and the income statement. What IS hydrated: the chart (`AccountRepository::allInOrder()`, a few hundred rows, for the group tree) and ONE bounded page of the journal report (the part-2 fetch-join `hydrate()`, reused — it now keeps the order of the ids the SQL chose).
- **Money from SQL**: MariaDB returns `SUM()` over `DECIMAL(15,2)` as a decimal STRING with two places; `LedgerReports::money()` turns it into `Money` with `Money::fromDecimal()` in the base currency (`LedgerService::baseCurrency()` — the ONE reader of `systemConfig → baseCurrency` in this module; the journal form's `journalCurrency()` delegates to it). No float anywhere on the path — a harness source guard (R46). Base currency only (ADR-042 decision 4).
- **`ReportRange`**: one fiscal year and from–to INSIDE it, both ends inclusive (`BETWEEN`); refused otherwise (`InvalidArgumentException`). A report never spans years.
- **Sign convention** (`AccountType::isDebitNormal()`): asset and expense are debit-normal, liability, equity and revenue credit-normal. Every balance a report shows is POSITIVE on the account's natural side and negative on the opposite side (an overdrawn bank account, a revenue reduction larger than the revenue). The trial balance does not sign at all: it splits the balance into «Saldo Soll» and «Saldo Haben».
- **Trial balance** (Saldobilanz): per account with lines in the range Σ Soll, Σ Haben, Saldo Soll / Saldo Haben; totals of both pairs, and `isBalanced()` (Σ Soll = Σ Haben AND Σ Saldo Soll = Σ Saldo Haben) shown as a badge.
- **Balance sheet** (Bilanz) is a statement AT a day: it reads from the year's FIRST day to «to» (`ReportRange::fromYearStart()`), whatever «from» says — the page offers «Stichtag» only. Aktiven = type `asset`; Passiven = Fremdkapital (`liability`) and Eigenkapital (`equity`) as two blocks; the CURRENT RESULT (revenue − expense over the same days) is a line of its own in Eigenkapital. With it, Aktiven = Passiven by construction of double entry (Σ(debit − credit) over all lines is zero); the page shows it and would show the difference.
- **Income statement** (Erfolgsrechnung) over from–to: Ertrag (`revenue`) and Aufwand (`expense`); result = Ertrag − Aufwand, positive a profit. At the balance sheet's day and from the year's start it equals the balance sheet's result line (harness R13).
- **Grouping** (`LedgerReports::section()`): the TYPE decides the block, the chart's parent chain the grouping. Each block holds the accounts of its types with lines, and every group on their parent chains with the SUBTOTAL of exactly those accounts, walked top-down from the roots in number order, depth for the indentation. A group without such an account is left out («groups without movement are hidden»); an account that moved but balances to zero is shown with 0.00. A KMU group mixing types (class 6 / group 69 with `6950 Finanzertrag`) appears in BOTH blocks, each time with its own accounts — no number rule. An account whose parent chain never reaches a root (a group cycle or an orphaned group written past the validator — a database edit, an import) is NOT dropped: it is appended flat at depth 0, listed in `StatementSection::unplaced`, and the block shows a note naming it; afterwards Σ of the depth-0 rows equals the block total (asserted — a mismatch is a code bug and throws).
- **Account statement** (Kontoblatt): one account, one range. `accountTotals()` gives in ONE query the opening (Σ debit − credit of the lines of the SAME fiscal year dated before «from»), Σ debit, Σ credit and the line count in the range; `accountLines()` one page in date / number / position order with the running Σ (debit − credit) as a WINDOW FUNCTION (`SUM(…) OVER (ORDER BY … ROWS BETWEEN UNBOUNDED PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW)`, MariaDB ≥ 10.2) computed over the whole range BEFORE `LIMIT/OFFSET` — so page 7's running balance counts pages 1–6 without reading them. Balance of a line = opening + running, on the natural side; the page's «Übertrag» (carry) = the first line's balance minus its own movement (= the opening on page 1; on a later page the row reads «Übertrag von Seite n» and carries no date); total and closing cover the whole range. Counter account (`counterAccounts()`, bounded by the page's entries): the one OTHER account of the entry with number and name, «div.» when there are several, «–» when none. The entry number links to the journal's detail page (`reportJournalBase()`).
- **Paging** (`Reports/Paging`, server links `?page=`, `.be-pagination`): account statement 500 lines per page (`LedgerReports::ACCOUNT_STATEMENT_PAGE_SIZE`), journal report 200 entries per page (`JOURNAL_PAGE_SIZE`). Chosen over a hard limit: a busy bank account has thousands of lines a year and every one must be reachable; 500 print as ~12 sheets. A page beyond the last (`?page=99`) shows the last page and a notice says so (`Paging::isBeyondLast()`), like the date fallbacks. The pager partial (`Backend/ReportController/pager`) stays in the fragment until a second consumer exists.
- **Journal report**: the entries of the range in date and number order with their lines, the count, Σ Soll and Σ Haben of the whole range — each summed on its own in SQL (`JournalEntryRepository::rangeSummary()`), the page shows both —, one page via `chronological()` (ids by SQL, then the part-2 fetch-join, which also fetches the reversed entry's fiscal year — «Storno von {year}/{number}» costs no query per reversal). Deleted manual entries are gaps; the journal SCREEN lists them from the change log.
- **The screens** (`/backend/finance/report/…`, fragment/host pattern like parts 1/2): `trial-balance` (the controller's `defaultAction` — `backendConfig` deviation entry, the reports have no list), `balance-sheet`, `income-statement`, `account-statement?account={number}`, `journal`. The tab row (`.be-viewtabs`, added by the trait to the shell's `tabs` slot) switches the report and keeps the range; hc2 switches the fiscal year (links with the year only, so a date of another year never arrives). Parameters are a GET form (`?year=` code — default the year containing today, else the latest: `FiscalYearRepository::currentOrLatest()`, the ONE rule the journal screen uses too —, `?from=` / `?to=` `YYYY-MM-DD`), the months of the year as shortcut links. A date that is not a date or lies outside the year, an unknown year, «from» after «to» → the default, and a German notice says so — never a 500, never a silent clamp. Read-only: no POST, no CSRF, no JavaScript (Rule 7). German labels, amounts through `AmountFormat`. The account number on every report links to its account statement over the same range.
- **Fragment slots — one pattern for every financial screen** (review 2026-09-22, L5): a fragment owns ALL of its templates, header slots and tab row included — `AccountController/addButton`, `FiscalYearController/openButton`, `JournalController/addButton` + `yearSwitch`, `ReportController/tabs` + `yearSwitch`. The trait adds them after `html()` with `$this->layoutManager->addPartials($name, 'Backend/{Controller}', {Layout}::NS, 'hc1'|'hc2'|'tabs')`. The host carries none (the backend's auto-loader for `{action}.hc1|hc2` files resolves in the backend namespace only, which would tie the slot to one mount); mounted elsewhere, the fragment brings its slots along (ADR-018, Rule 8). Covered by the harness through host doubles of the traits.
- **Print**: from the browser. The shell scrolls INSIDE column 2, so an unchanged print shows one viewport slice; `components/_shell.scss` therefore carries an `@media print` block (chrome hidden, shell as plain flow, `.be-noprint` for the form and the pager) — see `css-backend.md`. The only new CSS of part 3; no report has CSS of its own.
- **Volume** (plan §12/§13). Harness P: 10'000 entries / 20'000 lines SQL-inserted into one year — every report ≈ 0.1 s on the local MariaDB 10.6. Measured by the review (2026-09-22) at **200'000 lines**: trial balance and balance sheet ≈ 1.1 s, a journal page ≈ 0.8 s (filesort on date, number), an account statement of an account with 90'000 lines ≈ 1.6 s PER PAGE — the window function plus ORDER BY/LIMIT sorts the whole range on every page. EXPLAIN at 20'000 lines: `journal_entry` by `idx_journal_entry_date` (`fiscal_year_id, entry_date`), `journal_line` by the FK index on `entry_id`, `account` by primary key; whether MariaDB applies the account filter as a rowid filter or picks the `account_id` index depends on the volume and its statistics. A composite `journal_line (account_id, entry_id)` was measured and does NOT help (1.56 s). No new index or migration; the real fix is keyset pagination (pending, FIN-REPORT-005).

## rules

- When writing an `Account` from any code path (backend, import, a migration of a wdv chart) → MUST go through `AccountService` (`save()`, `update()`, `setActive()`); MUST NOT `persist()` an account directly — the validator lives there
- When changing an EXISTING (managed) account → MUST hand the cleaned values to `AccountService::update($account, $values)`, the parent as the entity under `parent`; MUST NOT call `mapFromArray()` or a setter on the loaded account first (ADR-039 decision 9)
- When an account is no longer needed → MUST deactivate it; MUST NOT delete the row or add a delete action — journal lines reference it
- When an account needs a different number → MUST create a new account and deactivate the old one; MUST NOT change `number` on an existing account (refused with `AccountNumberChangedException`)
- When an account's `type` or `postable` must change → MUST go through `AccountService::update()` OUTSIDE any open unit of work (the guarded path owns its unit of work and refuses a nested call); MUST NOT write `account.type` or `account.postable` in SQL or through a setter — the journal locks live in the service (FIN-TYPE-001)
- When the type of an account with lines in a `closed` period is wrong → MUST create a new account with the right type and move the balance with a manual transfer entry; MUST NOT reopen a period or edit the type to get around the refusal (owner, 2026-09-22: after the close nothing changes)
- When a code path writes journal lines → MUST re-check its accounts with `PostingRules::lockAccounts()` AFTER the number is drawn (`post()` does, the manual edit does without a number); MUST NOT take an account row lock BEFORE the `NumberRange` draw (ADR-039 decision 10) and MUST NOT rely on the plain read of `resolveAccounts()` alone
- When a caller of `post()` / `reverse()` receives `PostingRefusedException` → MUST let it end the unit of work (propagate or roll back); MUST NOT catch it inside `run()` and commit — `account-not-postable` / `account-inactive` can come AFTER the number was drawn (FIN-TYPE-001 race), and a commit would consume the number without an entry or a change row
- When grouping accounts → MUST put a postable account under a GROUP (`postable = false`); MUST NOT make an account with children postable or give a postable account children
- When offering the KMU chart → MUST call `AccountService::adoptKmuChart()` and offer the button only while `AccountRepository::isEmpty()`; MUST NOT seed the chart in the installer or a migration (owner, 2026-09-22 — a migrated chart must not get it forced on it)
- When editing `res/charts/kmu.json` → MUST keep UTF-8 without BOM, parents before children, five-type values and the Sterchi numbers, and MUST run `php tests/module-financial.php` (B-section checks the structure); MUST NOT round-trip it through Windows PowerShell (DATA-JSON-001)
- When a fiscal year is opened (backend, import, test) → MUST go through `FiscalYearService::open()` OUTSIDE any open unit of work (it refuses a nested call) and MUST treat a leftover `journal-entry.{code}` range as a conflict to resolve, not to reuse (refused on `code`); MUST NOT persist a `FiscalYear` or a `Period` directly or create `journal-entry.{code}` elsewhere — the year, its periods and its range are one unit of work
- When a fiscal year was opened by mistake → MUST delete it through `FiscalYearService::delete($yearId)` OUTSIDE any open unit of work, which allows only the LATEST year with no entry, no change row and its range at 0; MUST NOT delete a year, its periods or its range by hand in the database, MUST NOT delete a year that was ever posted in (correct it with entries instead), and MUST NOT drop `journal-entry.{code}` anywhere else
- When code needs the name of a year's journal-entry range → MUST use `FiscalYear::journalEntryRange()`; MUST NOT compose `'journal-entry.' . …` inline
- When choosing a fiscal-year code → MUST keep it lower-case kebab of at most 16 characters (it is the dotted segment of a range name); MUST NOT reuse a code or rely on case
- When a period's state has to change (VAT return, close — P5) → MUST add the transition to this module with its own rules (one way: `open` → `vat-settled` → `closed`, ADR-042 decision 10); MUST NOT write `fiscal_period.state` any other way
- When any module or script writes into the journal (an invoice, a payment, an import, a job) → MUST build a `PostingRequest::generated()` with an opaque origin and an idempotency key and call `LedgerService::post()` INSIDE its own `getTransaction(JournalEntry::class)->run()`, together with the writes that belong to the posting; MUST NOT `persist()` a `JournalEntry` or a `JournalLine` directly, MUST NOT draw from `journal-entry.{code}` itself, and MUST NOT open the unit of work inside financial (ADR-040 decisions 2 and 7, ADR-042 decision 6)
- When building an idempotency key → MUST derive it from the SOURCE (`invoice:{id}:final`, `payment:{id}`), stable across retries, and MUST repeat the identical content with it; MUST NOT reuse a key for different content — the ledger refuses it with `IdempotencyConflictException` instead of answering with the old entry
- When a posting has VAT → MUST put `taxCode`, `taxRate` (the rate that applied, from the document's snapshot), `taxBase` and `taxAmount` on the NET line only, signed as the document means them (a credit note negative); MUST NOT put tax data on the tax-account line or the counter line, and MUST NOT recompute the rate from the tables at posting time (ADR-041 decisions 6 and 7)
- When a generated entry is wrong → MUST call `LedgerService::reverse($ref, $date, $reason)` from the module that owns the source, inside its unit of work, with a reason of at most 255 characters (it becomes the reversal's text), dated in an OPEN period — the next fiscal year included; MUST NOT edit, delete or re-post it, and MUST NOT reverse a reversal or reverse twice (refused; the schema refuses the second reversal too)
- When a module's unit of work around `post()` / `reverse()` fails at commit with a `UniqueConstraintViolationException` on `uniq_journal_entry_idempotency` or `uniq_journal_entry_reversal_of` (a parallel poster with the same key, the same key twice in one unit of work, a parallel reversal) → MUST let the whole unit of work fail and propagate; MUST NOT catch it and retry inside the unit of work (ADR-039 decision 10) — no number was consumed; a retry is a new unit of work, and the same key then returns the existing entry. This binds the P3 debtor `AccountingGateway` adapter
- When a caller has no session identity (CLI script, import, harness) → MUST pass the actor's name to `LedgerService` / `ManualEntryService` explicitly; MUST NOT rely on `Actor::current()` there — it refuses rather than guesses
- When a MANUAL entry is created, changed or deleted (backend, script) → MUST go through `ManualEntryService::create()` / `update($entryId, $expectedVersion, $newRequest)` / `delete($entryId, $expectedVersion)` OUTSIDE any open unit of work (they own theirs, re-read and lock the entry, and log the change); MUST NOT call `JournalEntry::amend()`, `persist()` or `remove()` on an entry from anywhere else — the change log would miss the change
- When changing an EXISTING manual entry → MUST hand the NEW version as a `PostingRequest::manual()` together with the id and the VERSION the screen was rendered from (`JournalEntry::getVersion()`, the hidden `version` field); MUST NOT mutate a loaded entry or its lines (ADR-039 decision 9) and MUST NOT pass the version stored NOW instead of the one seen — that would defeat the lock
- When a screen or script receives `EntryConflictException` → MUST tell the user the entry was changed or deleted in the meantime and reload (the detail page or the list); MUST NOT retry with the current version automatically — the other writer's change has to be seen first
- When code checks whether a period accepts a write → MUST ask `PostingRules::assertPeriodAccepts()` (posting) or let `ManualEntryService` decide (edit/delete: old AND new period, tax line in old OR new version); MUST NOT re-implement the `open` / `vat-settled` / `closed` matrix elsewhere
- When a manual edit touches a line on an account that was deactivated since → MUST leave that line IDENTICAL (account, amounts, tax data, text) to keep it, or move it to an active account; MUST NOT expect a changed amount on an inactive account to pass (`account-inactive`)
- When a Doctrine entity of this module was loaded BEFORE a unit of work that rolled back → MUST re-read it through a repository before using it in a write; MUST NOT hand the detached object to a service (DOCTRINE-TX-004). The manual-entry services no longer take entities, so the UI path cannot run into this; a script that keeps its own objects across a failed unit of work still can
- When offering tax codes for a NEW manual line → MUST offer active codes only (`ManualEntryForm::selectableCodes()`); on an edit → MUST additionally allow the codes the entry already carries; MUST NOT let a deactivated code be newly chosen (ADR-043 decision 19)
- When showing an amount on a financial screen → MUST format it through `Ui/AmountFormat::of()`; MUST NOT cast a `Money` to float or call `number_format()` on it
- When the journal screen needs a list query → MUST add it to `JournalEntryRepository` (ids by SQL, hydration by a DQL fetch-join over lines and accounts, `latestForYear()` is the model) marked Doctrine-only; MUST NOT hydrate every entry of a year or let the template touch a lazy line collection per row
- When adding a column or an entity to this module → MUST run `php vendor/bin/z77-db diff --namespace="Z77\Module\Financial\Migrations"` and commit the reviewed migration with the entity, expand/contract; MUST NOT change `Version20260922071232` or `Version20260922091711` once an installation may have applied them
- When mounting the screens elsewhere → MUST `use AccountControllerTrait` / `FiscalYearControllerTrait` / `JournalControllerTrait` / `ReportControllerTrait`, delegate the layout config to `AccountLayout::config()` / `FiscalYearLayout::config()` / `JournalLayout::config()` / `ReportLayout::config()`, override `accountListBase()` / `fiscalYearListBase()` / `journalListBase()` / `reportBase()` (and `reportJournalBase()`, where the account statement links an entry to) to the mount's URL root, and give the report controller `defaultAction` `trial-balance`
- When a report needs numbers from the journal → MUST add an aggregate or a bounded read to `JournalLineRepository` (or `JournalEntryRepository` for a page of entries), on `connection()`, marked Doctrine-only, with EVERY value bound (dates as `Y-m-d`, LIMIT/OFFSET as `ParameterType::INTEGER`); MUST NOT hydrate `JournalLine`s, loop over entries of a year, or interpolate anything into the SQL
- When turning an amount from report SQL into money → MUST use `Money::fromDecimal()` in `LedgerService::baseCurrency()` (as `LedgerReports::money()` does); MUST NOT cast the decimal string to float, `round()` it or `number_format()` it — the harness source guard fails
- When a report shows a balance → MUST show it on the account's natural side via `AccountType::isDebitNormal()` (positive there, negative opposite), or split it into Saldo Soll / Saldo Haben like the trial balance; MUST NOT invent a per-report sign rule
- When building a statement block → MUST decide the side by the account TYPE and group by the parent chain (`LedgerReports::section()`); MUST NOT derive the side or the group from the account NUMBER (the KMU classes mix types)
- When a report needs balances from before the fiscal year (carry-forward) → MUST wait for the opening entry of P5 (plan §5.7); MUST NOT add up earlier years in a report as a workaround — the opening entry is derived from the previous year and stays re-derivable until the close (owner, FIN-REPORT-001)
- When a running balance must survive paging → MUST compute it in SQL over the whole range (the window function in `accountLines()`) and derive the page's carry from the first row; MUST NOT sum the previous pages in PHP or restart the balance per page
- When adding a report page → MUST add an action to `ReportControllerTrait` that goes through `reportPage()` (range parsing, notices, tab row, year switch) and a template under `Backend/ReportController/` that renders `rangeForm` with an EXPLICIT context; MUST NOT pass `get_defined_vars()` to a partial, add a form post, or add JavaScript
- When a report page must print → MUST rely on the shell's print rules and put screen-only parts in `.be-noprint`; MUST NOT add a print stylesheet or a print button
- When a financial screen needs a header slot or a tab row → MUST ship the partial in module-financial next to the screen's templates and add it from the trait with `addPartials(…, 'hc1' | 'hc2' | 'tabs')` after `html()`; MUST NOT add `{action}.hc1|hc2|tabs` files for it under the host's templates in module-backend
- When adding a method to this package → MUST have a production caller in the same change (CLAUDE.md «no just-in-case»); `accountExists()` arrives with the first configuration consumer (see pending)

## known issues

- **FIN-NAV-001** — don't expect «Kontenplan» / «Geschäftsjahre» / «Journal» / «Auswertungen» entries in the navigation after install: the kernel seed carries none (like CONTACT-NAV-001). The project adds them under the «Finanzen» section next to «MWST-Codes» («Auswertungen» → `/backend/finance/report`).
- **FIN-CHART-001** — don't assume the KMU chart is the full Sterchi chart: it is the classes, main groups, groups and the COMMON postable accounts (196 rows). An installation adds what its business needs (e.g. rounding accounts, plan §5.1 / §6.1) through the backend.
- **FIN-CHART-002** — don't assume the chart fits a sole proprietorship out of the box: class 28 is the variant for legal entities («Aktien-, Stamm-, Anteilschein- oder Stiftungskapital», reserves). Decided (owner, 2026-09-22): legal-entity variant only, no second chart file — a Personenunternehmen adds its equity accounts (Kapital, Privat) by hand in the backend after the adoption.
- **FIN-FY-001** — don't assume the proposed code is always free: two years in the same calendar year with the same shape (a short year 1.1.–31.3.2026 followed by 1.4.–31.12.2026) both propose `2026`; the second is refused on `code` and the user types another one (e.g. `2026-b`).
- **FIN-FY-002** — resolved 2026-09-22 (owner decision): a wrongly opened fiscal year is deleted in the backend («Löschen …» on the fiscal-year list) while it is the latest year and nothing was ever posted in it — `FiscalYearService::delete()`, see the mental model. What remains: don't expect to delete a year once anything was posted in it, also after the entry was deleted again (the change row documents the consumed number), or a year that is followed by another — there is no edit of a year's dates either; such a year stays and is corrected with entries.
- **FIN-FY-003** — don't expect two SIMULTANEOUS openings of the FIRST fiscal year (empty table) to end in a field error: both take only a gap lock in `lockAll()`, their inserts deadlock (InnoDB 1213), and the loser rolls back — surfacing as DBAL's `DriverException` 1305 with the `DeadlockException` down the `getPrevious()` chain (DOCTRINE-TX-005). `openAction()` recognises it (`RaceFailure::isDeadlock()`) and shows «Ein anderes Geschäftsjahr wurde gleichzeitig eröffnet — Liste neu laden» — no 500. This closes an integrity hole rather than opening one: before the lock (HEAD bea1fdc) both openings committed and the two first years could overlap. A caller of `FiscalYearService::open()` outside the backend (a script, the import) sees the exception; a retry is a new call, validated against the year that won.
- **FIN-TYPE-001** — resolved 2026-09-22 (owner decision): an account's `type` is locked once a journal line of it lies in a `closed` period, and a postable account cannot become a group once any line references it; name and active stay free — see the mental model. What remains: don't expect to correct a locked type in place — the correction is a new account plus a manual transfer entry; don't call `AccountService::update()` for a type or postable change inside your own unit of work (`LogicException`); and don't hold the account object after a refusal that came UNDER the lock (a race) — that unit of work rolled back, the EntityManager was replaced, the object is detached (re-read it by id, as the screen does).
- **FIN-IDEM-001** — don't assume the idempotency check sees a posting of the SAME unit of work: `findOneBy(['idempotencyKey' => …])` queries the database, and an entry `persist()`ed but not yet flushed is invisible to it. Two `post()` calls with the same key inside ONE `run()` both draw a number and the caller's commit fails on `uniq_journal_entry_idempotency` — loud, rolled back, no number consumed. Same for two parallel requests with the same key: the second commit fails on the index. A caller posts one key once per unit of work.
- **FIN-IDEM-002** — don't assume the idempotency comparison ignores presentation: the `text` and the line `text`s are part of the content. A module that rebuilds a posting text differently on a retry (a date formatted by locale, a changed template) gets `IdempotencyConflictException`; build the text from the source deterministically.
- **FIN-TX-001** — narrowed 2026-09-22 (review fix): the manual-entry services take id + version and re-read the entry inside their own unit of work, so the UI path never hands a detached entity to a write any more. What remains: don't hold a loaded `JournalEntry` across a unit of work that rolled back (also a refused NESTED `create()` / `update()` / `delete()`, which throws `LogicException` from inside `run()` and replaces the EntityManager) and then READ it for display only — that works; writing through it does not (DOCTRINE-TX-004). A script that keeps its own objects re-reads them.
- **FIN-LOCK-001** — don't assume a stale delete is caught by Doctrine's optimistic lock: Doctrine checks the version on UPDATE, not on DELETE. The delete path is safe because `ManualEntryService::delete()` re-reads the row under a lock (`lockForUpdate()`) and compares the version itself before `remove()` — a version check on the service, not on the statement. Do not bypass the service to delete an entry.
- **FIN-LOCK-002** — don't expect the conflict message to name what changed: `EntryConflictException` says the entry is gone or at another version; the user reloads and sees the current state and the change log. No merge, no diff — a manual entry is small, redoing the edit is the correction.
- **FIN-UI-001** — don't expect the manual form to compute the tax-account line: it computes and SHOWS the VAT of a taxed net line («MWST VM 8.1 %: 8.10 auf 100.00»); the bookkeeper enters the 1170 / 2200 line by hand, and the balance check refuses an entry that does not add up. Deliberate (no JavaScript, one write path — the ledger stores what it is given), not a gap.
- **FIN-UI-002** — don't expect the journal list to show every entry of a year: it stops at `journalListLimit` rows (default 200, newest first) and says «n von m angezeigt». No paging and no search there; the journal REPORT (`/backend/finance/report/journal`, paged) and the account statement of part 3 are where a full year is read.
- **FIN-REPORT-001** — don't expect carried-forward balances in a year after the first: there is no opening entry before P5 (plan §5.7), and every report reads the entries of ONE fiscal year. The balance sheet of a later year therefore shows only that year's movements — it is correct for the FIRST year, or when the bookkeeper posts a manual opening entry (Eröffnungsbuchung) on the first day. Deliberately not worked around by adding up earlier years: the opening entry is derived from the previous year and re-derivable until the close — P5 decides (owner). The balance sheet page says so on every year that is not the oldest; the account statement's opening balance is likewise the year's own lines before «from».
- **FIN-REPORT-002** — don't expect the balance sheet to honour «from»: it is a statement AT a day and always reads from the year's first day to «to» (`fromYearStart()`); the page offers «Stichtag» only. A «from» in its URL (from another tab) is ignored, not refused.
- **FIN-REPORT-003** — don't expect a whole year's account statement or journal on one printed run: they page (500 lines / 200 entries), and the browser prints the page on screen. Each printed account-statement page starts with its «Übertrag», so the pages chain; the totals line is on the last page. A one-run print of a whole year arrives with the export (pending).
- **FIN-REPORT-005** — don't expect the account statement to stay fast on a very large account: every page re-sorts the whole range for the window function (≈ 1.6 s per page for 90'000 lines of one account at 200'000 lines, measured 2026-09-22); an index does not change it. Acceptable for the volumes in sight; keyset pagination is the fix (pending), built when P5b shows real account volumes.
- **FIN-REPORT-004** — don't expect the report pages under an automated request check: like the journal screens, they are covered by the harness through a host double of the trait (context, sections, rendered templates), not by a real HTTP request; the print rules have no automated check at all (manual, pending).
- **FIN-UI-003** — don't look for a reverse button in the backend: reversal is `LedgerService::reverse()`, called by the module that posted the entry (ADR-042 decision 7). A generated entry a bookkeeper wants gone is corrected by that module — or, until debtor exists (P3), not at all through the UI.
